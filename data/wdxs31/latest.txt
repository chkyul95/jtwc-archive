CONVECTION PERSISTING ALONG THE SOUTHERN PERIPHERY OF A PARTIALLY
EXPOSED LOW-LEVEL CIRCULATION CENTER (LLCC). PREVIOUSLY, THE SYSTEM
WAS A SHARP TROPICAL WAVE, WITH 30-KT WINDS ON THE SOUTHERN SIDE
AND A LACK OF WESTERLY FLOW TO THE NORTH TO ESTABLISH A CLOSED
LOW-LEVEL CIRCULATION. SINCE THEN, CONVECTION HAS BECOME MORE
WIDESPREAD AND DEEPER. A 071707Z ASCAT-B PASS REVEALED GALE-FORCE
WINDS ALONG THE SOUTHERN PERIPHERY OF A CLOSED, HIGHLY ASYMMETRIC
LLCC. THEREFORE, THE SYSTEM HAS BEEN DESIGNATED A 35-KT TROPICAL
CYCLONE (TC) WITH HIGH CONFIDENCE. THE INITIAL POSITION AND WIND
RADII ARE ALSO ASSESSED WITH HIGH CONFIDENCE BASED ON THE
SCATTEROMETER DATA. THE ENVIRONMENT IS MARGINALLY FAVORABLE, WITH

NORTHERLY VERTICAL WIND SHEAR (VWS), AND STRONG POLEWARD OUTFLOW
ALOFT INTO A SUBTROPICAL JET TO THE SOUTHEAST.

3. FORECAST REASONING.

SIGNIFICANT FORECAST CHANGES: THIS INITIAL PROGNOSTIC REASONING
MESSAGE ESTABLISHES THE FORECAST PHILOSOPHY.

FORECAST DISCUSSION: THE EQUATORWARD EXTENSION OF A LOW- TO
MID-LEVEL RIDGE POSITIONED FAR TO THE SOUTHEAST WILL GENERALLY
STEER TC 03S NORTHWESTWARD, PASSING VERY NEAR THE NORTHERN TIP OF
MADAGASCAR. CONDITIONS WILL BECOME LESS FAVORABLE FOR
INTENSIFICATION AFTER TAU 12. TC 03S WILL MOVE OVER WATERS NEAR 26
DEGREES CELSIUS WHILE VWS REMAINS ELEVATED. CONCURRENTLY, THE
POLEWARD OUTFLOW CHANNEL WILL WEAKEN AS THE SYSTEM MOVES
NORTHWESTWARD. PROVIDED DEEP CONVECTION PERSISTS NEAR THE CENTER,
THE FORECAST CALLS FOR SLIGHT ADDITIONAL INTENSIFICATION THROUGH
TAU 12 BEFORE THE VORTEX ENCOUNTERS MORE HOSTILE CONDITIONS. BEYOND
TAU 24, VWS WILL INCREASE TO 20-25 KTS. INTERACTION WITH NORTHERN
MADAGASCAR, INCREASING VWS, AND WEAKENING OUTFLOW WILL RESULT IN
STEADY WEAKENING. TC 03S SHOULD DEGENERATE INTO A TROPICAL WAVE BY
TAU 48, WITH EARLIER DISSIPATION POSSIBLE IF THE CIRCULATION MOVES
OVER LAND.

MODEL DISCUSSION: TRACK GUIDANCE FANS OUT SUBSTANTIALLY THROUGH TAU
48, LIKELY DUE TO DIFFICULTY IN INITIALIZING THE ASYMMETRIC
CIRCULATION. GFS, UKMET, AND NAVGEM SHOW A RATHER SHARP EQUATORWARD
TURN, WHILE ECMWF AND GOOGLE DEEPMIND SHOW A MORE GRADUAL TURN. THE
LATTER GROUP APPEARS TO BE MORE CONSISTENT WITH THE INITIAL
POSITION AND TC STRUCTURE. ACCORDINGLY, THE JTWC FORECAST TRACK IS
PLACED ALONG THE SOUTHERN SIDE OF THE GUIDANCE ENVELOPE, NEAR THE
ECMWF AND DEEPMIND SOLUTIONS. INTENSITY GUIDANCE IS SPARSE, ALSO
LIKELY DUE TO DIFFICULTY AMONG THE MODELS IN RESOLVING A CLOSED
CIRCULATION. THE JTWC INTENSITY FORECAST IS SET NEAR COAMPS-TC AND
ECMWF, WHICH DEPICT STEADY INTENSITY OR SLIGHT INTENSIFICATION
THROUGH TAU 12 FOLLOWED BY STEADY WEAKENING.
