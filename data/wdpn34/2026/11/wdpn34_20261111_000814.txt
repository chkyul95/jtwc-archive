FORECAST DISCUSSION: TROPICAL DEPRESSION (TD) 15W IS EXPECTED TO
CONTINUE ON A PREDOMINANTLY NORTHWARD TRAJECTORY OVER THE NEXT 12
HOURS UNDER THE INFLUENCE OF THE CYCLONIC LOW-LEVEL FLOW OF THE
BROAD GYRE CIRCULATION SITUATED TO THE WEST. AFTER TAU 12, THE
REMAINING SHALLOW VORTEX APPROACHES EASTERLY LOW-LEVEL FLOW AND IS
EXPECTED TO TAKE ON A NORTHWESTWARD TRAJECTORY. THERE IS A BRIEF
PERIOD NEAR TAU 24 WHERE TD 15W MAY BE INFLUENCED BY TROPICAL STORM
(TS) 13W (PEILOU) AS BOTH SYSTEMS DISSIPATE IN THE HIGH SHEAR
ENVIRONMENT. NOW THAT THE LLCC HAS FULLY DECOUPLED FROM THE
MID-LEVELS, THE INTENSITY IS FORECAST TO STEADILY DECREASE IN AN
ENVIRONMENT THAT WILL BE HOSTILE TO ANY SUSTAINED CONVECTION. FULL
DISSIPATION OVER WATER IS EXPECTED BY TAU 24.

MODEL DISCUSSION: MODEL TRACK GUIDANCE HAS A FAIRLY WIDE SPREAD
OVER THE NEXT 24 HOURS BUT OVERALL THEY AGREE ON A PRIMARILY
NORTHWARD COMPONENT FOLLOWED BY A SHIFT NORTHWESTWARD. THE JTWC
TRACK IS PLACED CLOSE TO THE MULTI-MODEL CONSENSUS WITH MEDIUM
CONFIDENCE THROUGHOUT THE FORECAST PERIOD. THE INTENSITY GUIDANCE
REMAINS ON THE HIGHER END, WITH THE EXCEPTION OF HAFS-A WHICH
DEPICTS A MORE REALISTIC CHARACTERIZATION OF THE SHALLOW VORTEX.
THEREFORE, THE JTWC INTENSITY FORECAST IS PLACED CLOSER TO THE
HAFS-A SOLUTION WITH MEDIUM CONFIDENCE.
