3. FORECAST REASONING.

SIGNIFICANT FORECAST CHANGES: THERE ARE NO SIGNIFICANT CHANGES TO
THE FORECAST FROM THE PREVIOUS WARNING.

FORECAST DISCUSSION: TC 05B WILL TRAVEL NORTHWESTWARD AROUND THE
SOUTHWESTERN PERIPHERY OF THE STR. THIS DIRECTION OF MOTION WILL
CONTINUE UP TO AND THROUGH LANDFALL. VWS WILL STEADILY EASE OVER
THE NEXT 24-48 HOURS, WHILE VERY WARM SST AND MODERATE POLEWARD
OUTFLOW PERSIST. THESE CONDITIONS SHOULD ENABLE THE RATHER
DISORGANIZED LLCC OF TC 05B TO SLOWLY CONSOLIDATE. THE PEAK
INTENSITY FORECAST IS STILL UNCERTAIN. IF THE BROAD CIRCULATION CAN
QUICKLY DEVELOP AN INNER CORE, IT COULD INTENSIFY MORE QUICKLY AND
TO A HIGHER PEAK INTENSITY BEFORE THE ENVIRONMENT BECOMES LESS
FAVORABLE. AS TC 05B APPROACHES THE COASTLINE, NORTHEASTERLY SHEAR
WILL INCREASE, ENTRAINING DRY AIR FROM INDIA INTO THE CIRCULATION.
THE FORECAST CALLS FOR WEAKENING PRIOR TO LANDFALL. AFTER LANDFALL,
THE SYSTEM WILL RAPIDLY DECAY, DISSIPATING NEAR TAU 72.

MODEL DISCUSSION: THE TRACK GUIDANCE SUITE HAS SHIFTED POLEWARD
THIS CYCLE, AND THE JTWC FORECAST TRACK FOLLOWS SUIT. THE
CROSS-TRACK SPREAD IS LARGE, MEASURING 350 NM AT THE POINT OF
LANDFALL. GOOGLE DEEPMIND PROVIDES THE MOST POLEWARD SOLUTION,
FOLLOWED BY THE AI-MODEL MEAN (AICN), AND THE MULTI-MODEL CONSENSUS
(CONW). GFS AND ECMWF LIE NEAR CONW AND DEPICT LANDFALL SOUTH OF
VISAKHAPATNAM. THE JTWC FORECAST IS POSITIONED WITH LOW CONFIDENCE
BETWEEN AICN AND CONW AND HEDGES CLOSER TO AICN. INTENSITY GUIDANCE
IS WELL-CONSTRAINED, WITH A 10-KT SPREAD THROUGH TAU 48. HAFS-A
DEPICTS INTENSIFICATION TO 55 KTS, WHILE THE REST OF THE GUIDANCE
KEEPS THE INTENSITY AT 45-50 KTS. THE JTWC INTENSITY FORECAST LIES
NEAR THE INTENSITY CONSENSUS THROUGH TAU 72.
