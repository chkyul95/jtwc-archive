SATELLITE ANALYSIS, INITIAL POSITION AND INTENSITY DISCUSSION: 
ANIMATED MULTISPECTRAL SATELLITE IMAGERY (MSI) DEPICTS A BROAD, ILL-
DEFINED, AND HIGHLY ASYMMETRIC SYSTEM. PERSISTENT DEEP CONVECTION IS 
EVIDENT TO THE NORTH, CONTINUING TO DEVELOP ALONG A LOW-LEVEL 
CONVERGENT ASYMPTOTE SUPPORTED BY UPPER-TROPOSPHERIC DIFFLUENCE. THE 
LOW-LEVEL CIRCULATION CENTER (LLCC) REMAINS OBSCURED BY DENSE 
CONVECTIVE CIRRUS OUTFLOW IN BOTH THE SHORTWAVE INFRARED AND EARLY- 
DAY MULTISPECTRAL IMAGERY. THE ABSENCE OF RECENT HIGH-RESOLUTION 
MICROWAVE IMAGERY IS INHIBITING A DEFINITIVE ASSESSMENT OF THE LOW-
LEVEL INNER-CORE STRUCTURE, WHILE A PAUCITY OF RECENT SCATTEROMETER 
DATA PRECLUDES A COMPREHENSIVE ANALYSIS OF THE SYNOPTIC SURFACE WIND 
FIELD. AN 181845Z OSCAT-3 PARTIAL PASS DEPICTED AN ELONGATED WIND 
SPEED MINIMUM NEAR THE VORTEX CENTER, WITH WINDS OF 25-33 KNOTS 
CONCENTRATED ALONG THE EASTERN COAST OF INDIA BENEATH THE PERSISTENT 
CONVECTION IN THAT SECTOR. CONSISTENT WITH EARLIER ANALYSES, THE ECMWF 
AND EC-AIFS CONTINUE TO INITIALIZE THE SURFACE VORTEX SIGNIFICANTLY 
BETTER THAN THE GFS, DEPICTING A BROAD CORE AND ASYMMETRIC WIND FIELD 
WITH WINDS NOT EXCEEDING 30 KNOTS. THE INITIAL POSITION IS ASSESSED 
WITH LOW CONFIDENCE BASED ON AN EXTRAPOLATION OF THE OSCAT-3 AMBIGUITY 
DATA, AS WELL AS THE PGTW AND DEMS SATELLITE FIX POSITIONS. IN-SITU 
SURFACE OBSERVATIONS FROM BUOY 23092 TO THE NORTH (NORTHEASTERLY WINDS 
AT 27 KNOTS) AND BUOY 23459 TO THE SOUTH (SOUTHWESTERLY WINDS AT 21 
KNOTS) HELP TO CONSTRAIN THE INITIAL VORTEX POSITION. THE INITIAL 
INTENSITY IS ASSESSED WITH MEDIUM CONFIDENCE, HEDGED SLIGHTLY HIGHER 
THAN THE OSCAT-3 RETRIEVALS IN LIGHT OF THE HIGHER SUBJECTIVE AND 
OBJECTIVE INTENSITY ESTIMATES OUTLINED BELOW AND THE SCATTEROMETER GAP 
THAT MISSED THE QUADRANTS WHERE THE STRONGEST WINDS ARE LIKELY 
LOCATED. ENVIRONMENTAL CONDITIONS ARE MARGINAL, WITH HIGH SEA SURFACE 
TEMPERATURES (SSTS), LOW VERTICAL WIND SHEAR (VWS), AND STRONG 
POLEWARD OUTFLOW HEAVILY MITIGATED BY THE EXPANSIVE, DIFFUSE NATURE OF 
THE SYSTEM, WHICH EXHIBITS A RADIUS OF MAXIMUM WINDS (RMW) OF AT LEAST 
120 NM.

3. FORECAST REASONING.

SIGNIFICANT FORECAST CHANGES: THERE ARE NO SIGNIFICANT CHANGES TO
THE FORECAST FROM THE PREVIOUS WARNING.

FORECAST DISCUSSION: TROPICAL CYCLONE (TC) 05B HAS BEGUN TO ACCELERATE 
POLEWARD, HAVING REACHED AND ROUNDED THE STR AXIS. OVER THE NEXT 12 TO 
24 HOURS, THE SYSTEM IS FORECAST TO CONTINUE TRACKING NORTHWARD, 
STEADILY ACCELERATING ALONG THE WESTERN PERIPHERY OF THE RIDGE. AFTER 
TAU 24, TC 05B IS FORECAST TO BEGIN DECELERATING AS IT APPROACHES THE 
COASTLINE, WITH LANDFALL EXPECTED TO OCCUR ALONG THE EASTERN INDIAN 
COASTLINE NEAR PURI AND THE MAHANADI RIVER DELTA AROUND TAU 36. AFTER 
LANDFALL, THE SYSTEM WILL TRANSLATE STEADILY INLAND THROUGH THE 
REMAINDER OF THE FORECAST PERIOD. REGARDING INTENSITY, WHILE AMBIENT 
ENVIRONMENTAL CONDITIONS REMAIN FAVORABLE OVERALL, THE LACK OF A WELL-
DEFINED INNER CORE AND THE EXPANSIVE NATURE OF THE ASYMMETRIC WIND 
FIELD PRECLUDE SIGNIFICANT INTENSIFICATION. THE SYSTEM WILL LIKELY 
CONSOLIDATE SLIGHTLY BEFORE MAKING LANDFALL, AND WHILE MARGINAL 
INTENSIFICATION (5-10 KNOTS) IS POSSIBLE, THIS IS CONSIDERED UNLIKELY 
DUE TO THE LIMITED TIME REMAINING OVER OPEN WATER. THE FORECAST 
THEREFORE MAINTAINS A STEADY-STATE INTENSITY OF 35 KNOTS THROUGH 
LANDFALL. IF THE SYSTEM MAKES LANDFALL ALONG THE SATURATED MAHANADI 
RIVER DELTA, IT WILL LIKELY MAINTAIN INTENSITY BRIEFLY AFTER MOVING 
INLAND DUE TO SUSTAINED LATENT HEAT FLUXES OVER THE WETLAND TERRAIN 
(THE BROWN OCEAN EFFECT), BEFORE RAPIDLY WEAKENING AS IT TRACKS 
FURTHER INLAND OVER RUGGED TOPOGRAPHY AND DISSIPATES BY TAU 48. 

MODEL DISCUSSION: DETERMINISTIC AND ENSEMBLE TRACK GUIDANCE IS IN 
STRONG AGREEMENT THROUGH THE FORECAST PERIOD, WITH MINIMAL CROSS- OR 
ALONG-TRACK SPREAD AMONG THE MEMBERS OF THE CONSENSUS PACKAGE. THE 
OFFICIAL JTWC FORECAST IS PLACED NEAR THE OPERATIONAL GOOGLE DEEPMIND 
(GDMI), WHICH RESIDES NEAR THE MEDIAN OF THE GUIDANCE ENVELOPE. 
INTENSITY GUIDANCE IS BIFURCATED, WITH THE GFS-BASED MODELS (GFS, 
HAFS-A, AND HWRF) AS WELL AS COAMPS-TC (COTC) INDICATING 5-10 KNOTS OF 
INTENSIFICATION WITHIN THE NEXT 12 HOURS, FOLLOWED BY IMMEDIATE AND 
RAPID WEAKENING THROUGH THE REMAINDER OF THE FORECAST PERIOD. THE 
REMAINDER OF THE GUIDANCE DEPICTS A STEADY-STATE INTENSITY TREND 
THROUGH TAU 12, FOLLOWED BY GRADUAL WEAKENING THROUGH THE REMAINDER OF 
THE FORECAST PERIOD. THE JTWC INTENSITY FORECAST IS PLACED NEAR THE 
DECAY-SHIPS SOLUTION FOR THE DURATION OF THE FORECAST PERIOD.
