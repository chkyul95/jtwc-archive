3. FORECAST REASONING.

SIGNIFICANT FORECAST CHANGES: THERE ARE NO SIGNIFICANT CHANGES TO
THE FORECAST FROM THE PREVIOUS WARNING.

FORECAST DISCUSSION: THE BROAD, DISORGANIZED LLCC HAS BEEN
DIFFICULT TO TRACK, AND THE SHORT-TERM MOTION REMAINS UNCERTAIN. TC
05B WILL GENERALLY TRACK NORTHWESTWARD TO NORTH-NORTHWESTWARD ALONG
THE SOUTHWESTERN PERIPHERY OF THE STR. HOWEVER, THE BROAD NATURE OF
THE LLCC COULD SUPPORT ADDITIONAL ERRATIC SHORT-TERM MOTION AS THE
SYSTEM GRADUALLY GAINS LATITUDE. VWS APPEARS TO BE EASING AS CIRRUS
OUTFLOW BECOMES LESS RESTRICTED ALONG THE SOUTHEASTERN PERIPHERY.
EASING VWS, SST VALUES OF 28-29 DEGREES CELSIUS, AND MODERATE
POLEWARD OUTFLOW WILL PROVIDE TC 05B A SHORT WINDOW TO CONSOLIDATE.
ANY INTENSIFICATION WILL LIKELY BE SLOW AND LIMITED DUE TO THE
DISORGANIZED STORM STRUCTURE. NORTHEASTERLY SHEAR WILL BEGIN TO
INCREASE AFTER TAU 36 AND BECOME MORE PRONOUNCED NEAR TAU 48 AS THE
VORTEX APPROACHES LANDFALL. THIS INCREASING SHEAR WILL PROMOTE
DRY-AIR ENTRAINMENT FROM INDIA INTO THE CIRCULATION. THEREFORE, THE
JTWC INTENSITY FORECAST CALLS FOR WEAKENING TO COMMENCE AFTER TAU
36. AFTER LANDFALL, THE SYSTEM WILL QUICKLY DECAY, DISSIPATING NEAR
TAU 60.

MODEL DISCUSSION: THE SPREAD IN TRACK GUIDANCE REMAINS LARGE,
MEASURING 300 NM AT THE POINT OF LANDFALL, PARTLY DUE TO
DIFFICULTIES IN ASCERTAINING THE CENTER POSITION OF TC 05B. THE
GUIDANCE ENVELOPE SHIFTED POLEWARD OVER SEVERAL PRIOR CYCLES BUT
HAS NOT SHIFTED FURTHER POLEWARD THIS CYCLE. THE GOOGLE DEEPMIND
SOLUTION LIES NEAR THE POLEWARD EDGE OF THE GUIDANCE ENVELOPE,
WHILE THE GEFS ENSEMBLE MEAN, GALWEM, AND ECMWF LIE CLOSER TO THE
EQUATORWARD EDGE. THE JTWC FORECAST TRACK REMAINS SIMILAR TO THE
PREVIOUS FORECAST AND LIES SLIGHTLY EQUATORWARD OF THE AI-MODEL
MEAN (AICN). AT THE POINT OF LANDFALL, THE DEEPMIND SOLUTION LIES
APPROXIMATELY 110 NM NORTH OF THE JTWC FORECAST LANDFALL POINT.
INTENSITY GUIDANCE IS IN GOOD AGREEMENT, WITH THE BULK OF THE
SOLUTIONS MAINTAINING THE INTENSITY THROUGH TAU 24. HAFS-A AND HWRF
DEPICT WEAKENING BY TAU 36, WHILE DEEPMIND AND COAMPS-TC DELAY
WEAKENING UNTIL TAU 48. THE JTWC INTENSITY FORECAST LIES NEAR THE
INTENSITY CONSENSUS THROUGH TAU 60.
