SATELLITE ANALYSIS, INITIAL POSITION AND INTENSITY DISCUSSION: 
ANIMATED ENHANCED INFRARED (EIR) SATELLITE IMAGERY DEPICTS A VERY 
BROAD, ILL-DEFINED LOW-LEVEL CIRCULATION (LLC), WITH FLARING DEEP 
CONVECTION DRAPED ACROSS THE NORTHERN SEMICIRCLE, DEVELOPING ALONG A 
LOW-LEVEL CONVERGENCE BOUNDARY DISPLACED FROM THE VORTEX CENTER, AND 
WANING CONVECTION ACROSS THE SOUTHERN SEMICIRCLE. DUAL, PARTIAL ASCAT 
PASSES NEAR 181500Z INDICATED THAT THE STRONGEST SURFACE WINDS OF 30-
35 KNOTS WERE LOCATED TO THE NORTHWEST, ENHANCED BY COASTAL 
CONVERGENCE AND PINNED AGAINST THE EASTERN COAST OF INDIA, WHILE 25-
30-KNOT WINDS WERE EVIDENT ALONG THE EASTERN PERIPHERY. WEAK WINDS OF 
10-15 KNOTS WERE OBSERVED ACROSS THE SOUTHERN SEMICIRCLE. BASED ON THE 
ASCAT DATA AND SYNOPTIC MODEL ANALYSES, THE ECMWF AND EC-AIFS ARE 
INITIALIZING THE BROAD SURFACE WIND FIELD SIGNIFICANTLY BETTER THAN 
THE GFS. THE INITIAL POSITION IS ASSESSED WITH LOW CONFIDENCE BASED ON 
EXTRAPOLATION AND A SERIES OF LOW-RESOLUTION MICROWAVE PASSES, WHICH 
PROVIDED A SYNOPTIC VIEW OF THE CIRCULATION BUT LACKED THE SPATIAL 
RESOLUTION NECESSARY TO IDENTIFY A DISCRETE LOW-LEVEL CIRCULATION 
CENTER (LLCC). THE INITIAL INTENSITY IS ASSESSED WITH MEDIUM 
CONFIDENCE BASED ON THE OBJECTIVE AND SUBJECTIVE INTENSITY ESTIMATES 
OUTLINED BELOW AND ANALYSIS OF THE PARTIAL ASCAT PASSES NOTED ABOVE. 
ENVIRONMENTAL CONDITIONS ARE MARGINALLY FAVORABLE, CHARACTERIZED BY 
ROBUST POLEWARD OUTFLOW, HIGH SEA SURFACE TEMPERATURES (SSTS), AND LOW 
DEEP-LAYER VERTICAL WIND SHEAR (VWS). HOWEVER, THE EXPANSIVE, MONSOON-
DEPRESSION-LIKE NATURE OF THE CIRCULATION AND ABSENCE OF A 
CONSOLIDATED LLCC INHIBIT VORTEX SPIN-UP DESPITE THE OTHERWISE 
FAVORABLE AMBIENT CONDITIONS.

3. FORECAST REASONING.

SIGNIFICANT FORECAST CHANGES: THERE ARE NO SIGNIFICANT CHANGES TO
THE FORECAST FROM THE PREVIOUS WARNING.

FORECAST DISCUSSION: TROPICAL CYCLONE (TC) 05B HAS BEEN TRACKING 
SLOWLY NORTH-NORTHWESTWARD OVER THE PAST 48 HOURS, DECELERATING AS IT 
APPROACHES THE AXIS OF THE STR POSITIONED FAR TO THE EAST. OVER THE 
NEXT 12 TO 24 HOURS, TC 05B WILL REACH AND SUBSEQUENTLY ROUND THE 
RIDGE AXIS, ACCELERATING POLEWARD THROUGH THE REMAINDER OF THE 
FORECAST PERIOD. LANDFALL ALONG THE ODISHA COAST BETWEEN BRAHMAPUR AND 
PURI IS EXPECTED NEAR TAU 36, AFTER WHICH THE SYSTEM WILL TRANSLATE 
RELATIVELY QUICKLY INLAND. REGARDING INTENSITY, WHILE AMBIENT 
ENVIRONMENTAL CONDITIONS REMAIN FAVORABLE, THE VERY BROAD AND DIFFUSE 
VORTICITY FIELD WILL PRECLUDE SIGNIFICANT INNER-CORE CONSOLIDATION AND 
INTENSIFICATION PRIOR TO LANDFALL. SOME HIGH-RESOLUTION MESOSCALE 
MODELS INDICATE MARGINAL INTENSIFICATION, BUT LIMITED TO NO MORE THAN 
FIVE KNOTS. ONCE THE SYSTEM MAKES LANDFALL, TERRAIN-INDUCED SURFACE 
FRICTION AND LOSS OF OCEANIC ENTHALPY FLUX WILL QUICKLY SPIN DOWN THE 
VORTEX FROM BELOW, WHILE INCREASED VERTICAL WIND SHEAR WILL SHEAR IT 
FROM ABOVE, WEAKENING THE SYSTEM BELOW WARNING CRITERIA BY TAU 48. 

MODEL DISCUSSION: DETERMINISTIC AND ENSEMBLE TRACK GUIDANCE IS IN 
STRONG AGREEMENT, WITH ALL CONSENSUS MEMBERS INDICATING A GENERAL 
POLEWARD TRAJECTORY THROUGH THE FORECAST PERIOD. CROSS-TRACK SPREAD AT 
LANDFALL (TAU 36) IS 115 NM, BOUNDED BY THE GEFS MEAN ON THE WESTERN 
EDGE OF THE GUIDANCE ENVELOPE AND THE GOOGLE DEEPMIND (GDMI) ON THE 
EASTERN EDGE. THE OFFICIAL JTWC FORECAST IS PLACED NEAR THE AI 
CONSENSUS MEAN THROUGH THE FORECAST PERIOD WITH MEDIUM CONFIDENCE. 
INTENSITY GUIDANCE IS BIFURCATED, WITH THE GFS-BASED HAFS-A AND HWRF 
INDICATING RAPID INTENSIFICATION (RI) WITHIN THE NEXT 12 HOURS, WHILE 
THE REMAINDER OF THE GUIDANCE PACKAGE INDICATES EITHER STEADY-STATE 
INTENSITY OR GRADUAL WEAKENING THROUGH LANDFALL. THE JTWC INTENSITY 
FORECAST IS PLACED NEAR THE GDMI SOLUTION THROUGH THE FORECAST PERIOD, 
DISCOUNTING THE OVERLY AGGRESSIVE GFS-BASED MESOSCALE MODELS, WHICH 
UNREALISTICALLY FORECAST RAPID CONSOLIDATION OF THE LOW-LEVEL VORTEX 
GIVEN ITS CURRENT DIFFUSE STRUCTURE.
