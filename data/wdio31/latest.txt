3. FORECAST REASONING.

SIGNIFICANT FORECAST CHANGES: THERE ARE NO SIGNIFICANT CHANGES TO
THE FORECAST FROM THE PREVIOUS WARNING.

FORECAST DISCUSSION: TC 05B IS FORECAST TO BEGIN TRACKING
NORTHWESTWARD, ALONG THE WESTERN PERIPHERY OF THE STR TO THE EAST.
THE RIDGE TO THE EAST IS MUCH STRONGER THAN THE RIDGE TO THE WEST,
AND IS EXPECTED TO BECOME THE PRIMARY STEERING MECHANISM IN SHORT
ORDER. THE NORTHWESTWARD TRACK IS THEN EXPECTED TO PERSIST THROUGH
THE REMAINDER OF THE FORECAST PERIOD. LANDFALL IS CURRENTLY
FORECAST TO OCCUR BETWEEN TAU 60 AND TAU 72 ALONG THE EASTERN COAST
OF INDIA. THE LANDFALL LOCATION HAS BEEN STEADILY SHIFTING
NORTHWARD OVER THE PAST FEW WARNINGS, AND IS NOW FORECAST TO OCCUR
NORTHEAST OF VISAKHAPATNAM. REGARDING INTENSITY, 05B IS FORECAST
MAINTAIN ITS CURRENT INTENSITY THROUGH TAU 12 AS THE SOUTHEASTERLY
SHEAR REMAINS UNFAVORABLE. THE SYSTEM IS EXPECTED TO MARGINALLY
INTENSIFYING AFTERWARD AS THE SHEAR LESSENS. HOWEVER, THE BROAD
NATURE OF THE VORTEX WILL LIKELY LIMIT THE AMOUNT OF INTENSIFICATION. 
THEREFORE, AN INTENSITY OF ABOUT 45-50 KTS IS FORECAST NEAR TAU 48. 
THE ACTUAL PEAK COULD BE HIGHER IF THE SYSTEM IS ABLE TO CONSOLIDATE 
OR TUCK UNDERNEATH THE CONVECTION, BECOMING VERTICALLY ALIGNED. 
WESTERLY SHEAR IS ANTICIPATED TO INCREASE JUST BEFORE LANDFALL, 
CAUSING THE SYSTEM TO SLIGHTLY WEAKEN NEAR TAU 60. 05B WILL THEN 
FURTHER WEAKEN OVER LAND DUE TO TERRAIN INTERACTION, WITH DISSIPATION 
EXPECTED AROUND TAU 72 AS THE SHALLOW VORTEX DRIFTS FURTHER POLEWARD. 

MODEL DISCUSSION: TRACK GUIDANCE IS IN BETTER AGREEMENT THIS MODEL
RUN, WITH THE EXCEPTION OF THE GFS AND GEFS ENSEMBLE MEAN. THESE
TWO OUTLIERS ARE SIGNIFICANTLY FURTHER EQUATORWARD AND SLOWER THAN
THE REMAINDER OF GUIDANCE. DISCOUNTING THOSE TWO MODELS,
CROSS-TRACK SPREAD IS MEASURED TO BE ABOUT 160 NM AT TAU 48 AND 210
NM AT TAU 72. THE GOOGLE DEEPMIND AND EC-AIFS REMAIN THE MOST
POLEWARD MEMBERS WITHIN THE GUIDANCE ENVELOPE. THE JTWC TRACK
FORECAST IS PLACED NEAR THE MODIFIED CONSENSUS (REMOVING THE
OUTLIERS) AND THE AI-CONSENSUS WITH MEDIUM CONFIDENCE. INTENSITY
GUIDANCE IS IN SLIGHTLY WORSE AGREEMENT THIS RUN, PRIMARILY DUE TO
THE HAFS-A SHOWING A PEAK INTENSITY CLOSER TO 55-60 KTS WHILE THE
REMAINDER OF GUIDANCE IS WITHIN THE 40-50 KNOT RANGE. IT IS WORTH
NOTING THAT THE HAFS-A MODEL FIELDS SHOW THE VORTEX BECOMING NEARLY
FULLY VERTICALLY STACKED BEFORE LANDFALL. IF THAT SCENARIO DOES
INDEED PLAY OUT, THEN A HIGHER PEAK IS POSSIBLE. THE MAJORITY OF
MODELS KEEP THE VORTEX TILTED AND BROAD, HENCE THE JTWC PEAK
INTENSITY OF 45 KTS. OVERALL, THE JTWC INTENSITY FORECAST IS SET
NEAR THE CONSENSUS THROUGHOUT THE FORECAST PERIOD WITH MEDIUM
CONFIDENCE
