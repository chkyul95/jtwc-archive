FORECAST DISCUSSION: TC 04B IS LOCATED ALONG THE WESTERN PERIPHERY
OF THE STR AND IS TURNING MORE SHARPLY POLEWARD. THIS GENERALLY
POLEWARD MOTION WILL CONTINUE THROUGH TAU 24 AS THE SYSTEM MOVES

IS PREDICTED BY TAU 36.

MODEL DISCUSSION: SOME SPREAD EXISTS AMONG THE TRACK GUIDANCE. MOST
DYNAMICAL MODELS FAVOR AN EARLIER WESTWARD TURN, WHILE GFS AND
GOOGLE DEEPMIND DEPICT A MORE POLEWARD TRACK. THE JTWC FORECAST
TRACK IS POSITIONED NEAR THE GOOGLE DEEPMIND SOLUTION THROUGH TAU
24, THEN SHIFTS TOWARD THE MULTI-MODEL CONSENSUS BY TAU 36. ALL
INTENSITY AIDS INDICATE IMMINENT RAPID WEAKENING AS THE SYSTEM
MOVES INLAND. THE JTWC INTENSITY FORECAST IS ALIGNED WITH THE
INTENSITY AIDS THROUGH TAU 36.
