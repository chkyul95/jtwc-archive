WESTERN SEMICIRCLE, WITH THE ASCAT-C DATA INDICATING WINDS NEAR 40
KTS. SINCE THEN, THE CONVECTIVE PLUME AND ASSOCIATED POLEWARD
OUTFLOW HAVE EXPANDED SLIGHTLY. BASED ON THE SLIGHT IMPROVEMENT IN
SATELLITE APPEARANCE AND A BLEND OF THE T2.5-3.0 DVORAK
CLASSIFICATIONS FROM DEMS AND PGTW, THE INITIAL INTENSITY IS SET AT
40 KTS WITH MEDIUM CONFIDENCE. THIS ASSESSMENT ALIGNS WELL WITH THE
MAJORITY OF THE CIMSS OBJECTIVE INTENSITY ESTIMATES. THE INITIAL
POSITION IS PLACED WITH LOW CONFIDENCE NEAR THE PGTW FIX DUE TO THE
BROAD, PARTIALLY EXPOSED LLCC AND THE LACK OF RECENT MICROWAVE OR
SCATTEROMETER DATA. THE ENVIRONMENT IS MARGINAL, CHARACTERIZED BY

3. FORECAST REASONING.

SIGNIFICANT FORECAST CHANGES: THERE ARE NO SIGNIFICANT CHANGES TO
THE FORECAST FROM THE PREVIOUS WARNING.

FORECAST DISCUSSION: TC 05B HAS MOVED EAST-NORTHEASTWARD OVER THE
PAST SIX HOURS WITHIN A WEAK STEERING ENVIRONMENT. THIS SHORT-TERM
MOTION MAY SIGNAL A SHIFT AWAY FROM THE RECENT ERRATIC MOTION AS
THE BROAD CIRCULATION CONSOLIDATES. OVER THE NEXT 24 HOURS, A LOW-
TO MID-LEVEL STR TO THE EAST WILL SHIFT WESTWARD WHILE A SEPARATE
STR TO THE NORTHWEST RETREATS. TC 05B WILL CONSEQUENTLY TURN TOWARD
THE NORTHWEST AND APPROACH INDIA. THE ENVIRONMENT WILL SLOWLY
IMPROVE, WITH VWS EASING TO 15-20 KTS BY TAU 36 AND 10-15 KTS BY
TAU 60, WHILE SST REMAINS NEAR 28-30 DEGREES CELSIUS AND MODERATE
POLEWARD OUTFLOW PERSISTS. THESE CONDITIONS WILL FAVOR SLOW
INTENSIFICATION AT FIRST, PARTICULARLY WHILE THE VORTEX REMAINS
BROAD AND POORLY ORGANIZED UNDER STRONG SHEAR, FOLLOWED BY GRADUAL
TO STEADY INTENSIFICATION FROM TAU 48 TO TAU 72 AS VWS EASES
SUBSTANTIALLY. SHOULD TC 05B DEVELOP AN INNER CORE WITH SUFFICIENT
TIME PRIOR TO LANDFALL, RAPID INTENSIFICATION (RI) WILL BECOME
POSSIBLE. AFTER LANDFALL, THE SYSTEM WILL RAPIDLY DECAY, WITH
DISSIPATION OVER LAND OCCURRING NEAR TAU 96.

MODEL DISCUSSION: AGREEMENT AMONG TRACK GUIDANCE HAS WORSENED THIS
CYCLE. THE WEAKER SOLUTIONS, INCLUDING ECMWF AND GFS, FAVOR A MORE
ZONAL TRAJECTORY TOWARD INDIA. THE STRONGER SOLUTIONS, INCLUDING
GOOGLE DEEPMIND, COAMPS-TC, AND HAFS-A, ARE MORE POLEWARD. THIS
DIVERGENCE MAY STEM FROM DIFFERENCES IN THE DEPTH OF THE STEERING
FLOW, WHICH IS CLOSELY TIED TO STORM INTENSITY. THEREFORE, THE
CROSS-TRACK SPREAD AT THE POINT OF LANDFALL SPANS 370 NM. THE
AI-MODEL MEAN (AICN) LIES EQUATORWARD OF DEEPMIND BUT POLEWARD OF
THE MULTI-MODEL CONSENSUS (CONW). THE JTWC FORECAST TRACK IS
SIMILAR TO THE PREVIOUS FORECAST, POSITIONED BETWEEN AICN AND CONW
BUT HEDGED CLOSER TO AICN. INTENSITY GUIDANCE IS IN FAIR AGREEMENT.
COAMPS-TC AND HAFS-A DEPICT FASTER INITIAL INTENSIFICATION AND PEAK
NEAR 60 KTS. GFS HOLDS STEADY AT 40-45 KTS. DEEPMIND SHOWS SLOW
INITIAL INTENSIFICATION FOLLOWED BY STEADY STRENGTHENING AFTER TAU
36. THE JTWC INTENSITY FORECAST ROUGHLY FOLLOWS THIS LATTER TREND,
LYING NEAR THE LOWER END OF THE GUIDANCE ENVELOPE THROUGH TAU 36
AND APPROACHING THE HIGHER END BY TAU 72. THE DEEPMIND ENSEMBLE
ILLUSTRATES THE SENSITIVITY TO INNER-CORE DEVELOPMENT, WITH
INTENSITIES RANGING FROM 40 KTS AT THE 10TH PERCENTILE TO 85 KTS AT
THE 90TH PERCENTILE NEAR THE POINT OF LANDFALL.
