3. FORECAST REASONING.

SIGNIFICANT FORECAST CHANGES: THERE ARE NO SIGNIFICANT CHANGES TO
THE FORECAST FROM THE PREVIOUS WARNING.

FORECAST DISCUSSION: TC 05B HAS INCREASED TRACK SPEEDS IN THE
NORTHWESTWARD DIRECTION OVER THE LAST 12 HOURS AS THE STRONG
RIDGING TO THE EAST ASSERTS DOMINANCE AS THE PRIMARY STEERING
MECHANISM FOR THE SYSTEM. THE NORTHWESTWARD TRACK IS EXPECTED TO
PERSIST FOR ANOTHER 12 HOURS BEFORE THE TRAJECTORY BECOMES MORE
NORTHWARD AS 05B TRAVERSES THE WESTERN EXTENT OF THE RIDGE AXIS.
THE LANDFALL TIMING AND LOCATION IS SIMILAR TO THE PREVIOUS
FORECAST, AND IS EXPECTED TO OCCUR BETWEEN TAU 36 AND TAU 48. IN
TERMS OF INTENSITY, 05B IS FORECAST TO MARGINALLY INTENSIFY OVER
THE NEXT 12 HOURS AS THE WIND FIELD CONSOLIDATES AND SHEAR EASES.
THE BROAD NATURE OF THE SYSTEM WILL LIKELY HINDER ANY CHANCES FOR
SIGNIFICANT DEVELOPMENT. NEAR TAU 36, WESTERLY SHEAR WILL QUICKLY
INCREASE, CAUSING THE SYSTEM TO BEGIN WEAKENING JUST BEFORE
LANDFALL. THE STRONGEST WINDS ARE ANTICIPATED TO REMAIN LOCATED
ALONG THE NORTHERN SEMICIRCLE OF THE CIRCULATION THROUGHOUT THE
FORECAST PERIOD. SOON AFTER LANDFALL, 05B IS FORECAST TO DISSIPATE
OVER LAND AS STRONG SHEAR AND TERRAIN INTERACTION ERODE THE SHALLOW
VORTEX. 

MODEL DISCUSSION: TRACK GUIDANCE IS IN BETTER AGREEMENT IN THIS MODEL
RUN WITH THE CROSS-TRACK SPREAD AT TAU 48 MEASURED TO BE ABOUT 180
NM. WHEN DISCOUNTING THE GEFS MEAN, WHICH IS THE WESTERNMOST MODEL,
THE SPREAD DECREASES TO JUST 110 NM. AS A RESULT, THE JTWC TRACK
FORECAST IS PLACED CLOSE TO THE MULTI-MODEL CONSENSUS THROUGHOUT
THE SHORT FORECAST PERIOD WITH HIGH CONFIDENCE. INTENSITY GUIDANCE
GENERALLY, AGREES ON A STEADY INTENSITY OR SLIGHT INTENSIFICATION
THROUGH TAU 24, FOLLOWED BY WEAKENING. HWRF IS SOMEWHAT OF AN
AGGRESSIVE OUTLIER IN THIS MODEL RUN, SUGGESTING A PEAK INTENSITY OF
AROUND 65 KTS AT TAU 24. THE REMAINDER OF GUIDANCE SITS WITHIN THE
40-50 KNOT RANGE. THE JTWC INTENSITY FORECAST CLOSELY FOLLOWS THE
CONSENSUS WITH MEDIUM CONFIDENCE.
