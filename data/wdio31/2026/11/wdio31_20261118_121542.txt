SIGNIFICANT FORECAST CHANGES: THERE ARE NO SIGNIFICANT CHANGES TO

FORECAST DISCUSSION: TC 05B IS FORECAST TO IMMINENTLY BEGIN PICKING
UP SPEED IN THE NORTHWESTERN DIRECTION AS THE STEERING PATTERN
BECOMES DOMINATED BY THE STRONG RIDGE TO THE EAST. THE
NORTHWESTWARD TRAJECTORY WILL THEN PERSIST THROUGH TAU 24 WHERE 05B
IS EXPECTED TO TURN MORE POLEWARD AS IT ROUNDS THE WESTERN EXTENT
OF THE RIDGE. LANDFALL ALONG THE EASTERN COAST OF INDIA IS
CURRENTLY FORECAST TO OCCUR JUST BEFORE TAU 48 WITH THE LANDFALL
LOCATION SHIFTING MORE NORTHEASTWARD COMPARED TO THE PREVIOUS
FORECAST. REGARDING INTENSITY, 05B IS FORECAST TO MARGINALLY
INTENSIFY OVER THE NEXT 12 HOURS AS THE WIND FIELD CONSOLIDATES AND
SOUTHEASTERLY SHEAR CONTINUES TO EASE. THE SYSTEM IS FORECAST TO
THEN MAINTAIN AN INTENSITY OF AROUND 45 KTS THROUGH TAU 36 AS IT
APPROACHES THE COAST. FOLLOWING TAU 36, WESTERLY SHEAR WILL QUICKLY
INCREASE, CAUSING THE SYSTEM TO WEAKEN JUST BEFORE LANDFALL. 05B IS
FORECAST TO DISSIPATE OVER LAND NO LATER THAN TAU 60 AS THE SHALLOW
VORTEX TRACKS FURTHER INLAND. 

MODEL DISCUSSION: MODEL TRACK GUIDANCE GENERALLY AGREES ON THE

MEASURED TO BE ABOUT 220 NM AT TAU 48. THE GEFS MEAN IS FURTHER
EQUATORWARD MODEL WHILE THE GOOGLE DEEPMIND REMAINS THE FURTHER
POLEWARD. THE JTWC TRACK FORECAST IS PLACED CLOSE TO THE
MULTI-MODEL CONSENSUS THROUGHOUT THE FORECAST PERIOD WITH MEDIUM
CONFIDENCE. INTENSITY GUIDANCE AGREES ON A STEADY INTENSITY OR
MARGINAL INTENSIFICATION THROUGH TAU 36 FOLLOWED BY WEAKENING WITH
NO NOTABLE OUTLIERS PRESENT. THE JTWC INTENSITY FORECAST IS ALSO
PLACED NEAR THE MULTI-MODEL CONSENSUS WITH MEDIUM CONFIDENCE.
