3. FORECAST REASONING.

SIGNIFICANT FORECAST CHANGES: THERE ARE NO SIGNIFICANT CHANGES TO
THE FORECAST FROM THE PREVIOUS WARNING.

FORECAST DISCUSSION: TC 05B IS FORECAST TO BEGIN TRACKING
NORTHWESTWARD, ALONG THE WESTERN PERIPHERY OF THE STR TO THE EAST.
THE RIDGE TO THE EAST IS STRONGER THAN THE RIDGE TO THE WEST, AND
IS EXPECTED TO BECOME THE PRIMARY STEERING MECHANISM IN SHORT
ORDER. THE NORTHWESTWARD TRACK IS EXPECTED TO PERSIST THROUGH THE
REMAINDER OF THE FORECAST PERIOD. LANDFALL IS CURRENTLY FORECAST TO
OCCUR RIGHT AROUND TAU 72 ALONG THE EASTERN COAST OF INDIA.
REGARDING INTENSITY, 05B IS FORECAST TO START INTENSIFYING AROUND
TAU 12-24 AS THE SOUTHEASTERLY SHEAR LESSENS. HOWEVER, THE BROAD
NATURE OF THE VORTEX WILL LIKELY LIMIT THE AMOUNT OF
INTENSIFICATION. THEREFORE, AN INTENSITY OF ABOUT 50 KTS IS
FORECAST NEAR THE TIME OF LANDFALL. THE ACTUAL PEAK COULD BE HIGHER
IF THE SYSTEM IS ABLE TO CONSOLIDATE CONSIDERABLY BEFORE LANDFALL.
05B WILL THEN RAPIDLY WEAKEN OVER LAND, WITH DISSIPATION EXPECTED
NO LATER THAN TAU 96. 

MODEL DISCUSSION: TRACK GUIDANCE IS IN POOR AGREEMENT WITH THE
CROSS-TRACK SPREAD MEASURED TO BE ABOUT 450 NM AT TAU 72. THE GEFS
ENSEMBLE MEAN, AND ECMWF ENSEMBLE MEAN REMAIN THE MOST EQUATORWARD
MODELS, WHILE THE GOOGLE DEEPMIND AND EC-AIFS ARE THE MOST POLEWARD.
THE JTWC TRACK FORECAST IS PLACED SLIGHTLY POLEWARD OF THE
MULTI-MODEL CONSENSUS THROUGHOUT THE FORECAST PERIOD WITH LOW
CONFIDENCE. INTENSITY GUIDANCE AGREES ON GRADUAL INTENSIFICATION
FROM TAU 24 UP UNTIL LANDFALL WITH NO SIGNIFICANT OUTLIERS PRESENT.
AS A RESULT, THE JTWC INTENSITY FORECAST IS PLACED NEAR THE
CONSENSUS WITH MEDIUM CONFIDENCE.
