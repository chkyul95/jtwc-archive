FORECAST DISCUSSION: THE RECENT MOTION OF TC 05B HAS BEEN ERRATIC
AS THE BROAD CIRCULATION CONTINUES TO CONSOLIDATE WITHIN A WEAK
STEERING ENVIRONMENT. OVER THE NEXT 24 HOURS, A LOWER-LEVEL RIDGE
TO THE EAST WILL STRENGTHEN AND BUILD WESTWARD, WHILE THE SEPARATE
STR TO THE NORTHWEST RETREATS. TC 05B WILL CONSEQUENTLY TURN TOWARD
THE NORTHWEST. THIS GENERAL MOTION WILL CONTINUE THROUGH LANDFALL
BETWEEN TAU 72 AND TAU 96. THE BROAD CIRCULATION, LARGE CORE OF
LIGHT WINDS, AND PERSISTENT VWS WILL LIMIT SIGNIFICANT
INTENSIFICATION IN THE SHORT TERM. VWS WILL EASE DURING THE 24-36
HOURS PRIOR TO LANDFALL, WHILE SST WILL REMAIN ABOVE 28 DEGREES
CELSIUS. THEREFORE, JTWC CALLS FOR SLOW INTENSIFICATION THROUGH TAU
36, FOLLOWED BY A SLIGHTLY FASTER RATE OF INTENSIFICATION TO A PEAK
OF 50 KTS BY TAU 72. ONCE TC 05B MOVES INLAND, RAPID WEAKENING WILL
FOLLOW, WITH DISSIPATION OCCURRING NEAR OR SHORTLY AFTER TAU 96.
