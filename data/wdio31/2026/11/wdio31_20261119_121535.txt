ANIMATED MULTISPECTRAL SATELLITE IMAGERY (MSI) DEPICTS TROPICAL
CYCLONE (TC) 05B (AKVAN) WITH A PARTIALLY EXPOSED LOW LEVEL
CIRCULATION CENTER (LLCC) WITH CONTINUED ROBUST CONVECTION
DISPLACED TO THE NORTH OF THE SYSTEM. A 190338Z METOP-B ASCAT PASS
REVEALED A SLIGHTLY ELONGATED LLCC DEVOID OF ANY 30-35 KT WINDS ON
THE EASTERN OR SOUTHERN FLANK OF THE SYSTEM, HOWEVER THE AREA OF
MAXIMUM WINDS ARE ASSESSED TO REMAIN WITHIN THE NORTHWESTERN
QUADRANT AS PREVIOUS WIND SPEED DATA HAS SHOWN, NOT CAPTURED BY
THIS MOST RECENT ASCAT PASS. ENVIRONMENTAL CONDITIONS ARE ASSESSED
AS MARGINALLY FAVORABLE, AS THERE REMAINS LOW TO MODERATE (10-15
KTS) OF VERTICAL WIND SHEAR, WARM (28-29 C) SEA SURFACE
TEMPERATURES, AND STRONG POLEWARD OUTFLOW ALOFT, HOWEVER THE
SHEARED AND BROAD NATURE OF THE LLCC IS ASSESSED TO CONTINUE
OFFSETTING FURTHER INTENSIFICATION. THE INITIAL POSITION IS PLACED
WITH MEDIUM CONFIDENCE BASED ON THE PARTIALLY EXPOSED NATURE OF THE
LLCC. THE INITIAL INTENSITY IS ASSESSED WITH LOW CONFIDENCE, AS THE
SYSTEM IS LIKELY BORDERLINE OF 30-35 KTS BASED ON THE SPREAD OF
AGENCY DVORAK FIXES AND CIMSS OBJECTIVE ESTIMATES THAT HOVER AROUND
THE TROPICAL STORM THRESHOLD.

3. FORECAST REASONING.

SIGNIFICANT FORECAST CHANGES: THERE ARE NO SIGNIFICANT CHANGES TO
THE FORECAST FROM THE PREVIOUS WARNING.

FORECAST DISCUSSION: TC 05B IS FORECAST TO TRACK NORTHWARD FOR THE
REMAINDER OF THE FORECAST PERIOD AS IT ROUNDS THE AXIS OF THE
SUBTROPICAL RIDGE (STR) CENTERED TO THE EAST. LANDFALL IS FORECAST
TO OCCUR JUST AFTER TAU 24 BETWEEN BRAHMAPUR AND PURI AND THE
SYSTEM IS EXPECTED TO CONTINUE NORTHWARD AFTER LANDFALL. REGARDING
INTENSITY, A PLATEAU OF 35 KTS IS FORECAST OUT TO TAU 24, AFTER
WHICH TERRAIN INTERACTION IS EXPECTED TO DISSIPATE THE SHALLOW
VORTEX BY TAU 36. DISSIPATION PRIOR TO TAU 36 IS POSSIBLE AS THE
CIRCULATION REMAINS BROAD UP UNTIL LANDFALL AS INITIALIZED BY ECMWF
AND AIFS GLOBAL MODEL FIELDS, WHILE GFS IS THE ONLY GLOBAL MODEL
FIELD TO CONSTRICT THE CIRCULATION AND SHOW FURTHER INTENSIFICATION
AFTER TAU 6, LENDING UNCERTAINTY TO THE INTENSITY FORECAST IS IT
POTENTIALLY OSCILLATES ABOUT 35 KTS OF INTENSITY.

MODEL DISCUSSION: TRACK MODEL GUIDANCE IS IN GOOD AGREEMENT FOR THE
REMAINDER OF THE FORECAST PERIOD, WITH THE GEFS AND AI GEFS MAKING
UP THE EASTERN AND WESTERN MOST SOLUTIONS, CAUSING A CROSS-TRACK
SPREAD OF 110 NM AT TAU 36. THE OFFICIAL JTWC FORECAST TRACK IS
PLACED WITH HIGH CONFIDENCE, CLOSE TO THE MULTI-MODEL AND AI
CONSENSUS. INTENSITY MODEL GUIDANCE IS IN FAIR AGREEMENT WITH ALL
MAINTAINING 05B AT 35 KTS OUT TO TAU 12 TO 24. THE OFFICIAL JTWC
FORECAST INTENSITY IS ASSESSED WITH MEDIUM CONFIDENCE, SLIGHTLY
ABOVE THE BULK OF GUIDANCE PACKAGE AT TAU 24 TO ACCOUNT FOR THE
POSSIBLE RE-INTENSIFICATION FORECAST BY GFS, HAFS-A, AND HWRF.
