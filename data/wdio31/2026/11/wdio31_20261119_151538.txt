3. FORECAST REASONING.

SIGNIFICANT FORECAST CHANGES: THERE ARE NO SIGNIFICANT CHANGES TO
THE FORECAST FROM THE PREVIOUS WARNING.

FORECAST DISCUSSION: TC 05B IS FORECAST TO CONTINUE TRACKING
NORTHWARD ALONG THE PERIPHERY OF A SUBTROPICAL RIDGE CENTERED TO
THE EAST OF THE SYSTEM, CAUSING A SLIGHT EASTWARD TURN AFTER TAU 24
AS IT BECOMES POLEWARD OF THE STR AXIS. THE SYSTEM IS EXPECTED TO
MAKE LANDFALL JUST AFTER TAU 24, NEAR BRAHMAPUR. REGARDING
INTENSITY, THE SYSTEM IS FORECAST TO MAINTAIN 35 KTS OUT TO TAU 12
AND BEGIN WEAKENING THEREAFTER AS 05B NEARS LANDFALL AND BEGINS
INTERACTING WITH THE TERRAIN. POTENTIAL PASSAGE OVER THE CHILIKA
LAKE AND SOUTHERN PORTION OF THE MAHANADI RIVER DELTA COULD
MARGINALLY OFFSET INITIAL WEAKENING FROM TERRAIN INTERACTION, AS
THE BROWN-OCEAN EFFECT ALLOWS 05B TO ABSORB LATENT HEAT FROM THE
WARM, SATURATED TERRAIN. HOWEVER, THIS IS EXPECTED TO BE SHORT-LIVED, 
AS DISSIPATION IS ULTIMATELY FORECAST TO OCCUR NO LATER THAN TAU 36 AS
05B PROCEEDS FURTHER INLAND. WHILE NOT WITHIN THE OFFICIAL
FORECAST, THERE REMAINS A POSSIBILITY FOR MORE RAPID DISSIPATION
DUE TO THE LOW CONFIDENCE IN THE CURRENT INTENSITY AND ASSESSED
BORDERLINE TROPICAL-STORM STRENGTH.

MODEL DISCUSSION: TRACK MODEL GUIDANCE REMAINS IN FAIR AGREEMENT,
ALTHOUGH CROSS-TRACK SPREAD SHARPLY INCREASES TO 150 NM AT TAU 36
AS MODELS STRUGGLE TO TRACK THE VORTEX OVER LAND. A FEW MODELS
(EXPERIMENTAL WEATHER MESH, AIFS, ECMWF, GDMN) DISSIPATE THE SYSTEM
BETWEEN TAUS 12 AND 24. THERE IS STRONG CONFIDENCE IN THE TIMING OF
LANDFALL, AS ALL MODELS HAVE THE SYSTEM OVER THE COAST AT TAU 24
WITH AN ALONG-TRACK SPREAD OF 50 NM AND A CROSS-TRACK SPREAD 70 NM
(DISCOUNTING NAVGEM AS AN OUTLIER). THUS, THE OFFICIAL JTWC
FORECAST TRACK IS PLACED WITH HIGH CONFIDENCE, IN LINE WITH THE
MULTI-MODEL AND AI CONSENSUS. INTENSITY MODEL GUIDANCE IS IN BETTER
AGREEMENT, WITH ONLY HWRF AND HAFS-A DEPICTING A 5 KT
INTENSIFICATION AT TAU 12, WHILE THE REST PLATEAU OR IMMINENTLY
WEAKEN THE SYSTEM. THE OFFICIAL JTWC FORECAST INTENSITY IS ASSESSED
WITH MEDIUM CONFIDENCE, PLACED SLIGHTLY ABOVE THE MULTI-MODEL
CONSENSUS AFTER TAU 24 TO REFLECT THE LESS-SHARP WEAKENING TREND OF
DECAY SHIPS, BASED ON ITS DEMONSTRATED SKILL IN REGARDS TO SYSTEMS
MAKING LANDFALL.
