FORECAST DISCUSSION: TY 31W WILL CONTINUE TO ACCELERATE EAST-
NORTHEASTWARD DURING THE FORECAST PERIOD IN THE FLOW BETWEEN A MID-
LATITUDE TROUGH TO THE NORTHWEST AND A DEEP-LAYER SUBTROPICAL RIDGE 
(STR) CENTERED TO THE EAST. IMMINENT INTERACTION WITH A BAROCLINIC 
FRONTAL BOUNDARY WILL DRIVE STEADY DEFORMATION OF THE STORM 
CIRCULATION. THE CLOSED CIRCULATION WILL DEGENERATE INTO AN OPEN 
FRONTAL WAVE AND DISSIPATE AS A DISCRETE TROPICAL CYCLONE PRIOR 
TO COMPLETING EXTRATROPICAL TRANSITION. STEADY WEAKENING IS 
ANTICIPATED AS THE SYSTEM PASSES OVER COOLER WATER AND CONTENDS WITH 
RAPID DRY AIR ENTRAINMENT. HOWEVER, SUFFICIENT BAROCLINIC SUPPORT AND 
WRAPPING OF PRE-EXISTING GALE FORCE AND STRONGER WINDS ALONG THE 
FRONTAL BOUNDARY SHOULD ENABLE THE SYSTEM TO MAINTAIN PEAK LOW-LEVEL 
WIND SPEEDS AT OR NEAR STORM FORCE THROUGHOUT THE 24-HOUR FORECAST 
PERIOD.

MODEL DISCUSSION: DETERMINISTIC AND ENSEMBLE TRACK GUIDANCE IS IN 
STRONG AGREEMENT THROUGH THE ABBREVIATED FORECAST PERIOD. THE PHYSICS 
BASED AND MACHINE LEARNING (ML) MODEL CONSENSUSES ARE TIGHTLY PACKED 
AND THE CURRENT JTWC FORECAST IS CONSISTENT WITH BOTH SOLUTIONS AS 
WELL AS THE PREVIOUS FORECAST. INTENSITY GUIDANCE IS ALSO IN STRONG 
AGREEMENT, INDICATING CONTINUED WEAKENING BUT MAINTENANCE OF ABOVE 
GALE-FORCE WINDS OVER THE NEXT 24 HOURS.
