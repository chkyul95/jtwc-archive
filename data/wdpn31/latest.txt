3. FORECAST REASONING.

SIGNIFICANT FORECAST CHANGES: THE FORECAST HAS BEEN SHORTENED TO 24
HOURS. 

FORECAST DISCUSSION: TYPHOON (TY) 31W IS TRANSLATING RAPIDLY EAST-
NORTHEASTWARD ALONG THE NORTHWESTERN PERIPHERY OF THE DEEP-LAYER STR 
TO THE EAST AND AHEAD OF THE RAPIDLY APPROACHING LOWER-TROPOSPHERIC 
COLD FRONTAL BOUNDARY. NO SIGNIFICANT CHANGE IS EXPECTED IN THE 
OVERALL TRAJECTORY OF TY 31W, OTHER THAN CONTINUED TRANSLATIONAL 
ACCELERATION WITHIN THE STRENGTHENING MID-LATITUDE WESTERLIES THROUGH 
THE END OF THE FORECAST PERIOD. WITHIN THE NEXT 12 HOURS, TY 31W WILL 
BEGIN TO INTERACT WITH THE BAROCLINIC FRONTAL BOUNDARY AND INITIATE 
EXTRATROPICAL TRANSITION (ETT). HOWEVER, AS THE SYSTEM BECOMES 
EMBEDDED WITHIN THE FRONTAL BAROCLINIC ZONE BY TAU 24, THE CLOSED LLCC 
WILL DEGENERATE INTO AN OPEN FRONTAL WAVE AND DISSIPATE AS A DISCRETE 
TROPICAL CYCLONE PRIOR TO COMPLETING ETT. REGARDING INTENSITY, TY 31W 
WILL STEADILY WEAKEN UNDER THE COMBINED HOSTILE FORCING OF EXTREME 
VERTICAL WIND SHEAR, PERVASIVE DRY AIR ENTRAINMENT, AND DECREASING 
SSTS OVER THE NEXT 24 HOURS. HOWEVER, WHILE TY 31W WILL DEGENERATE 
INTO AN OPEN FRONTAL WAVE ALONG THE COLD FRONT, IT IS FORECAST TO 
RETAIN AN EXPANSIVE, BAROCLINICALLY ENHANCED WIND FIELD OF GALE- TO 
STORM-FORCE WINDS AS IT DISSIPATES. 

MODEL DISCUSSION: DETERMINISTIC AND ENSEMBLE TRACK GUIDANCE IS IN 
STRONG AGREEMENT THROUGH THE ABBREVIATED FORECAST PERIOD, WITH MINIMAL 
CROSS-TRACK SPREAD INCREASING TO JUST 75 NM, BOUNDED BY NAVGEM ON THE 
NORTHERN EDGE AND GALWEM ON THE SOUTHERN EDGE OF THE GUIDANCE 
ENVELOPE, THROUGH TAU 24. ALONG-TRACK SPREAD GRADUALLY INCREASES TO 
JUST UNDER 100 NM BY TAU 24, WITH THE DATA-DRIVEN AI MODELS OUTPACING 
THE BULK OF THE DYNAMICAL PHYSICS-BASED GUIDANCE. THE OFFICIAL JTWC 
FORECAST IS POSITIONED NEAR THE AI CONSENSUS MEAN WITH HIGH 
CONFIDENCE. INTENSITY GUIDANCE IS ALSO IN STRONG AGREEMENT, DEPICTING 
STEADY WEAKENING THROUGH THE FORECAST PERIOD. THE JTWC INTENSITY 
FORECAST IS PLACED NEAR THE HAFS-A SOLUTION THROUGH TAU 24 WITH HIGH 
CONFIDENCE.
