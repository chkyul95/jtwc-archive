SATELLITE ANALYSIS, INITIAL POSITION AND INTENSITY DISCUSSION:
ANIMATED MULTISPECTRAL SATELLITE IMAGERY (MSI) DEPICTS TYPHOON (TY)
31W (CEMPAKA) WITH A CENTRAL DENSE OVERCAST (CDO) OFFSET TO THE
NORTHEAST OF THE LOW LEVEL CIRCULATION CENTER (LLCC). AN 110532Z
F-18 MICROWAVE SUITE CONFIRMS THE ASYMMETRICAL NATURE OF THE
CONVECTIVE STRUCTURE WITH THE MICROWAVE EYE FEATURE IN THE 37 GHZ
IMAGE ERODED IN THE SOUTHWESTERN QUADRANT AND THE BULK OF STRONGER
CONVECTION EASTWARD AND POLEWARD OF THE LLCC. 31W HAS BEGUN
INTERACTION WITH THE COLD FRONT TO THE NORTHWEST AS A SWATH OF
DRIER AIR CONTINUES TO WRAP AROUND THE WESTERN FLANK, AND THE
PARTIALLY EXPOSED LLCC CONTINUES TO ELONGATE. ENVIRONMENTAL CONDITIONS 
ARE ASSESSED AS UNFAVORABLE AS 31W IS SITUATED OVER THE 26 C ISOTHERM 
AND VERTICAL WIND SHEAR HAS RISEN TO OVER 30 KTS. AS 31W NEARS THE
UPPER-LEVEL JET, POLEWARD OUTFLOW ALOFT REMAINS ROBUST. THE INITIAL
POSITION IS PLACED WITH HIGH CONFIDENCE BASED ON THE WELL-RESOLVED
MICROWAVE EYE FEATURE IN THE SSMIS IMAGERY. THE INITIAL INTENSITY
IS ASSESSED WITH MEDIUM CONFIDENCE AS THE MAJORITY OF AGENCY DVORAK
FIXES SUGGEST A SYSTEM JUST AT TYPHOON-STRENGTH WHILE CIMSS
OBJECTIVE ESTIMATES AVERAGE SLIGHTLY LOWER.

INITIAL WIND RADII BASIS: PERSISTENCE

3. FORECAST REASONING.

SIGNIFICANT FORECAST CHANGES: THERE ARE NO SIGNIFICANT CHANGES TO
THE FORECAST FROM THE PREVIOUS WARNING.

FORECAST DISCUSSION: 31W HAS BEGUN INTERACTION WITH THE COLD FRONT
SITUATED TO THE NORTHWEST OF THE SYSTEM, CAUSING THE ONSET OF
EXTRATROPICAL TRANSITION (ETT) AS 31W LOSES SOME TROPICAL
CHARACTERISTICS. BEFORE ETT CAN COMPLETE HOWEVER, FURTHER
INTERACTION WITH THE FRONTAL BOUNDARY WILL CAUSE THE CIRCULATION OF
31W TO INSTEAD OPEN AND DISSIPATE INTO THE LARGER FRONTAL FEATURE
BY TAU 24. THE ENTIRE SYSTEM WILL CONTINUE ACCELERATING
NORTHEASTWARD AS IT REMAINS BETWEEN THE STEERING GRADIENT OF THE
MID-LATITUDE TROUGH TO THE NORTHWEST AND THE SUBTROPICAL RIDGE
CENTERED TO THE EAST. REGARDING INTENSITY, 31W IS EXPECTED TO
CONTINUE STEADILY WEAKENING AS VWS INCREASES, SSTS DECREASE, AND
COLDER, DRY AIR FROM THE COLD FRONT ENTRAINS INTO THE CORE. DESPITE
THE FORECAST WEAKENING, 31W IS EXPECTED TO MAINTAIN AN EXPANSIVE
WIND FIELD OF GALE FORCE WINDS AT THE TIME IT DISSIPATES.

MODEL DISCUSSION: TRACK MODEL GUIDANCE IS IN FAIR AGREEMENT ON AN
ACCELERATING NORTHEASTWARD TRACK FOR THE REMAINDER OF THE FORECAST
PERIOD BUT HAS SPREAD IN COMPARISON TO THE PREVIOUS CYCLE. A FEW
MODELS (ECMWF, BOTH GOOGLE DEEPMINDS) LOSE THE VORTEX AFTER TAU 12
WHILE THE NORTHERN AND SOUTHERNMOST OUTLIERS OF NAVGEM AND GALWEM
RESULT IN A CROSS-TRACK AND ALONG-TRACK SPREAD OF 200 NM
(DISREGARDING NAVGEM AND GALWEM) AT TAU 24. THE OFFICIAL JTWC
FORECAST TRACK IS PLACED WITH MEDIUM CONFIDENCE, IN LINE WITH THE
MULTI-MODEL CONSENSUS AND SLIGHTLY SLOWER THAN THE SI-BASED
CONSENSUS. MODEL INTENSITY GUIDANCE IS IN FAIR AGREEMENT WITH
CONTINUED WEAKENING, EXCLUDING NAVGEM-BASED COAMPS-TC WHICH SHOWS
RE-INTENSIFICATION OUT TO TAU 12. THE OFFICIAL JTWC INTENSITY
FORECAST IS ASSESSED WITH HIGH CONFIDENCE, IN LINE WITH THE
MULTI-MODEL CONSENSUS.
