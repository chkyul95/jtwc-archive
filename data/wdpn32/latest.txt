3. FORECAST REASONING.

SIGNIFICANT FORECAST CHANGES: FORECAST INTENSITIES HAVE DECREASED
AND THE INITIAL PORTION OF THE FORECAST TRACK HAS SHIFTED EASTWARD
SLIGHTLY FROM THE PREVIOUS FORECAST DUE TO OBSERVED POSITION AND
STRUCTURAL CHANGES.

FORECAST DISCUSSION: AFTER INITIALLY POLEWARD MOTION, TY 29W WILL
TURN NORTH-NORTHEASTWARD AROUND THE PERIPHERY OF THE CURRENT
SUBTROPICAL STEERING RIDGE THROUGH THE FORECAST PERIOD. EXTRATROPICAL 
TRANSITION WILL BEGIN BY TAU 36 AS THE SYSTEM INTERACTS WITH A 
DEVELOPING MID-LATITUDE TROUGH TO THE NORTHWEST. TY 29W WILL MERGE 
WITH THE TROUGH AND BECOME FULLY EMBEDDED BY TAU 60. STEADY WEAKENING 
WILL OCCUR PRIOR TO EXTRATROPICAL TRANSITION DUE TO ONGOING 
ENTRAINMENT OF DRIER AIR FROM THE WEST, PASSAGE OVER MARGINAL (25-26C) 
SEA SURFACE TEMPERATURES, AND INCREASING VERTICAL WIND SHEAR. 

MODEL DISCUSSION: NUMERICAL MODEL TRACK FORECAST GUIDANCE REMAINS
IN GOOD AGREEMENT REGARDING THE OVERALL SCENARIO. THE PHYSICS-BASED
AND AI MODEL CONSENSUSES ARE SPREAD BY ABOUT 50 NM FROM TAU 24
ONWARD, WITH THE AI CONSENSUS SITUATED TO THE SOUTH AND BOTH DEPICTING
SIMILAR TRACK SPEEDS. THE CURRENT JTWC FORECAST MAINLY SPLITS THE
DIFFERENCE BETWEEN THE TWO CONSENSUS SOLUTIONS AND, ALTHOUGH LYING
A BIT TO THE EAST EARLY ON DUE TO RECENT STORM MOTION, IS
CONSISTENT WITH THE PREVIOUS JTWC TRACK FORECAST AFTER TAU 24.
INTENSITY FORECAST GUIDANCE IS IN VERY GOOD AGREEMENT REGARDING A
CONTINUED STEADY WEAKENING TREND AND FLATLINE TREND FOLLOWING
EXTRATROPICAL TRANSITION AS THE SYSTEM MERGES INTO A MID-LATITUDE
TROUGH AND LIKELY BECOMES INDISTINGUISHABLE AS A SEPARATE
CIRCULATION.
