ASSESSED WITH HIGH CONFIDENCE BASED ON AN EXTRAPOLATION OF THE WSF-M

3. FORECAST REASONING.

SIGNIFICANT FORECAST CHANGES: THERE ARE NO SIGNIFICANT CHANGES TO
THE FORECAST FROM THE PREVIOUS WARNING.

FORECAST DISCUSSION: TROPICAL CYCLONE (TC) 04P IS DRIFTING SLOWLY  
POLEWARD WITHIN A WEAK STEERING PATTERN WHILE EXHIBITING SHORT-TERM 
TROCHOIDAL OSCILLATIONS. THE FUTURE TRAJECTORY OF TC 04P IS TIGHTLY 
COUPLED WITH ITS INTENSITY EVOLUTION AND VERTICAL VORTEX DEPTH. IN THE 
NEAR-TERM, AS THE SYSTEM REMAINS QUASI-STATIONARY, SELF-INDUCED 
OCEANIC UPWELLING WILL INDUCE A GRADUAL WEAKENING TREND THROUGH TAU 
24. BY TAU 36, DEEP-LAYER NORTHWESTERLY SHEAR BEGINS TO INCREASE 
SHARPLY, RESULTING IN VERTICAL VORTEX DECOUPLING BETWEEN TAU 36 AND 
TAU 48. AS THE VORTEX REDUCES IN VERTICAL EXTENT, THE SYSTEM WILL 
TRANSITION TO THE STEERING INFLUENCE OF THE LOW- TO MID-LEVEL EAST-
NORTHEASTERLY SYNOPTIC FLOW AND DEFLECT SHARPLY SOUTHWESTWARD BY TAU 
48. TC 04P THEN ACCELERATES SOUTHWESTWARD THROUGH THE REMAINDER OF THE 
FORECAST PERIOD, TRACKING ALONG THE NORTHERN PERIPHERY OF THE
LOW- TO MID-LEVEL STR BEFORE TURNING SLIGHTLY MORE POLEWARD BY THE
END OF THE FORECAST PERIOD AHEAD OF AN APPROACHING MID-LATITUDE 
FRONTAL SYSTEM. THERMAL UPWELLING WILL INDUCE APPROXIMATELY 10 KNOTS 
OF WEAKENING OVER THE NEXT 24 HOURS, WITH RAPID WEAKENING EXPECTED 
AFTER TAU 48 DUE TO VORTEX DECOUPLING AND SEVERE MID-TROPOSPHERIC DRY 
AIR ENTRAINMENT VENTILATING THE REMNANT LOW-LEVEL VORTEX. TC 04P IS 
FORECAST TO COMMENCE DISSIPATION BY TAU 120 AND COULD POTENTIALLY 
WEAKEN BELOW WARNING CRITERIA BETWEEN TAU 96 AND TAU 120.   

MODEL DISCUSSION: DETERMINISTIC AND ENSEMBLE TRACK GUIDANCE IS IN GOOD 
OVERALL AGREEMENT ON THE SYNOPTIC TRACK SCENARIO, THOUGH SIGNIFICANT 
BIFURCATION REMAINS REGARDING THE POST-INFLECTION TRAJECTORY. WITHIN 
THE TRACK CONSENSUS, CROSS-TRACK SPREAD INCREASES TO 150 NM BY TAU 72 
AND 410 NM BY TAU 120. BY TAU 120, THE WESTERNMOST OUTLIERS ARE THE 
EGRR AND UKMET ENSEMBLE, PLACING THE VORTEX CENTER NEAR THE WESTERN 
ISLAND OF SAVAII (SAMOA), WHILE THE EASTERN OUTLIER IS THE ECMWF 
ENSEMBLE MEAN FAR EAST OF THE MANUA ISLANDS, WITH THE REMAINING 
CONSENSUS MEMBERS ROUGHLY EQUALLY DISPERSED ALONG AN AXIS THROUGH 
SAMOA AND AMERICAN SAMOA. THE DETERMINISTIC ECMWF, GOOGLE DEEPMIND 
(GDMI), AI CONSENSUS (AICN), AND THE JTWC CONSENSUS (CONW) ALL TRACK 
EAST OF AMERICAN SAMOA, WHILE THE REMAINDER OF THE GUIDANCE PACKAGE 
TRACKS TO THE WEST. ENSEMBLE GUIDANCE DEPICTS SIGNIFICANT SPREAD, WITH 
VERY HIGH INTRA-ENSEMBLE DISPERSION PARTICULARLY AFTER TAU 48, WITH 
INDIVIDUAL MEMBERS DIVERGING ANYWHERE FROM TUVALU TO THE NORTHERN COOK 
ISLANDS. THE OFFICIAL JTWC FORECAST IS POSITIONED NEAR THE AI 
CONSENSUS WITH MEDIUM CONFIDENCE THROUGH TAU 72 AND LOW CONFIDENCE 
THEREAFTER. INTENSITY GUIDANCE IS IN GOOD AGREEMENT, WITH ALL MEMBERS 
INDICATING A STEADY WEAKENING TREND THROUGH TAU 24, FOLLOWED BY A MORE 
RAPID WEAKENING PHASE FROM TAU 48 ONWARDS. THE JTWC INTENSITY FORECAST 
IS PLACED NEAR THE GDMI SOLUTION THROUGH THE FORECAST PERIOD WITH 
MEDIUM CONFIDENCE.
