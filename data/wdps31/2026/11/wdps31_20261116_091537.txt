SATELLITE ANALYSIS, INITIAL POSITION AND INTENSITY DISCUSSION:
ANIMATED ENHANCED INFRARED (EIR) SATELLITE IMAGERY DEPICTS TROPICAL
CYCLONE (TC) 04P (XAVIER) WITH AN INCREASINGLY SYMMETRICAL COMPACT
CORE, WITH CONVECTION NOW FULLY WRAPPED AROUND, OBSCURING THE LOW 
LEVEL CIRCULATION CENTER (LLCC). A 160544Z GPM MICROWAVE SUITE 
REVEALED A FURTHER DEVELOPED MICROWAVE EYE FEATURE IN BOTH THE 37 GHZ 
AND THE 89 GHZ IMAGES, WITH THE DEEPEST CONVECTIVE BANDING POSITIONED  
ACROSS THE WESTERN AND NORTHERN FLANKS, WHILE THE STRONGER RAINBANDS 
BEYOND THE DISCRETE EYE FEATURE REMAIN INCOMPLETE AND BROKEN. THE EYE 
FEATURE IN THE 89 GHZ IMAGE IS SITUATED NORTHWEST OF THE LLCC, 
INDICATIVE OF VORTEX PRECESSION AS THE UPPER LEVEL CIRCULATION ORBITS 
THE LLCC CYCLONICALLY AS IT ATTEMPTS TO IMPROVE VERTICAL ALIGNMENT. 
ENVIRONMENTAL CONDITIONS ARE ASSESSED AS FAVORABLE, WITH LOW (10-15 
KTS) VERTICAL WIND SHEAR (VWS), WARM (29-30 C) SEA SURFACE 
TEMPERATURES (SSTS), AND STRONG WESTWARD AND POLEWARD OUTFLOW ALOFT 
DUE TO A POINT SOURCE POSITIONED TO THE EAST OF THE SYSTEM. THE 
INITIAL POSITION IS ASSESSED WITH HIGH CONFIDENCE BASED ON THE TIMELY 
MICROWAVE DATA. THE INITIAL INTENSITY IS ASSESSED WITH MEDIUM 
CONFIDENCE BASED ON THE HIGHER END OF AGENCY DVORAK FIXES AND CIMSS 
OBJECTIVE ESTIMATES AS THE IMPROVED CONVECTIVE STRUCTURE OF XAVIER 
INDICATES THE LIKELIHOOD OF RAPID INTENSIFICATION (RI).

INITIAL WIND RADII BASIS: PERSISTENCE 

CURRENT STEERING MECHANISM: COMBINATION OF A LOW TO MID LEVEL

3. FORECAST REASONING.

SIGNIFICANT FORECAST CHANGES: THERE ARE NO SIGNIFICANT CHANGES TO
THE FORECAST FROM THE PREVIOUS WARNING.

FORECAST DISCUSSION: XAVIER IS FORECAST TO TRACK WEST-SOUTHWESTWARD
FOR THE NEXT 24 HOURS UNDER THE COMBINED STEERING INFLUENCE OF THE
LOW TO MID LEVEL SUBTROPICAL RIDGE (STR) CENTERED TO THE SOUTH AND
THE WESTERN EXTENSION OF A DEEP-LAYER STR CENTERED TO THE
SOUTHEAST. AS THE SYSTEM INTENSIFIES AND DEEPENS IN VERTICAL
EXTENT, STEERING INFLUENCE IS EXPECTED TO BECOME DOMINATED BY THE
DEEP-LAYER STR, CAUSING A POLEWARD TURN BY TAU 36 AS 04P NEARS THE
AXIS OF THE WESTERN EXTENSION. THIS WILL BE ACCOMPANIED BY A
FURTHER DECREASE IN THE ALREADY SLOW TRACK SPEED, AS XAVIER ENTERS A
COL REGION BETWEEN THE EASTERN STR AND A SECOND, DISTANT STR
CENTERED FAR TO THE WEST. 04P IS EXPECTED TO BECOME
QUASI-STATIONARY WITHIN THIS COL REGION UNTIL AROUND TAU 96, AT
WHICH POINT ITS DECREASE IN INTENSITY WILL CAUSE XAVIER TO BE
STEERED PRIMARILY WEST-NORTHWESTWARD BY THE LOW-LEVEL EASTERLY TRADES.
REGARDING INTENSITY, THE COMPACT NATURE AND IMPROVED STRUCTURE OF THE 
CORE AS WELL AS CONTINUED FAVORABLE ENVIRONMENTAL CONDITIONS ARE 
INDICATIVE OF FURTHER RAPID INTENSIFICATION (RI) OUT TO TAU 24, WITH 
AN ULTIMATE PEAK INTENSITY OF 95 KTS EXPECTED TO OCCUR BETWEEN TAUS 36 
AND 48 AS THE APPROACH TO THE COL REGION BETWEEN STRS OPTIMIZES 
OUTFLOW ALOFT. AFTER THIS PEAK, STEADY WEAKENING IS EXPECTED FOR THE 
REMAINDER OF THE FORECAST PERIOD AS VWS BECOMES INCREASINGLY HOSTILE, 
ENTRAINING DRY AIR INTO THE CORE. ADDITIONALLY, OCEAN HEAT CONTENT 
(OHC) IS EXPECTED TO DECREASE, COINCIDING WITH THE STALL IN TRACK 
SPEED, INDUCING THE UPWELLING OF COOLER SSTS.

MODEL DISCUSSION: MODEL TRACK GUIDANCE HAS COME INTO SIGNIFICANTLY
BETTER AGREEMENT, WITH ALL PHYSICS AND SI-BASED MODELS NOW
DEPICTING A POLEWARD TURN AT TAU 36. BEYOND TAU 72, THERE REMAINS
SOME SPREAD AS TO HOW MUCH THE SYSTEM WILL RECURVE AROUND THE AXIS
OF THE STR EXTENSION BEFORE TURNING SHARPLY WESTWARD ONCE MORE,
WITH GEFS AS THE WESTERNMOST OUTLIER DEPICTING A DIRECT SOUTHWARD
TRACK BEFORE TURNING WESTWARD WHEREAS HAFS-A AND AIFS DO NOT DEPICT
THE WESTWARD TURN AT ALL, RATHER MAINTAINING 04P ON A SOUTHEASTWARD 
TRACK AT TAU 120. OVERALL, CROSS TRACK SPREAD IS 300 NM AND ALONG 
TRACK SPREAD IS 210 NM AT TAU 120 AS MODELS STRUGGLE TO RESOLVE THE
EFFECTS OF THE WEAKENED STEERING ENVIRONMENT ON THE TRACK OF XAVIER
IN LATER TAUS. THE OFFICIAL JTWC FORECAST TRACK IS PLACED WITH HIGH
CONFIDENCE OUT TO TAU 72 AND MEDIUM CONFIDENCE THEREAFTER, IN GOOD
AGREEMENT WITH THE MULTI MODEL AND SI CONSENSUS THROUGHOUT. MODEL
INTENSITY GUIDANCE REMAINS SPLIT WITH BOTH GOOGLE DEEPMINDS, ECMWF
AND HAFS-A EXPLICITLY FORECASTING RI (ALONG WITH SEVERAL RI AIDS
THAT CONTINUE TO POPULATE) WHILE THE REMAINDER OF MESOSCALE MODELS
AND GFS MAKE UP THE LOWER END OF THE GUIDANCE PACKAGE, SHOWING A
PEAK INTENSITY OF 65-75 KTS. THE BULK OF GUIDANCE CONVERGE INTO
BETTER AGREEMENT BY TAU 84 AS ALL SHOW WEAKENING, WITH THE
EXCEPTION OF HAFS-A THAT SHOWS A FURTHER PEAK OF 135 KTS AND HAS
BEEN EXCLUDED AS AN OUTLIER. THE OFFICIAL JTWC INTENSITY FORECAST
IS ASSESSED WITH MEDIUM CONFIDENCE THROUGHOUT, CLOSELY IN LINE WITH
GOOGLE DEEPMIND, ECMWF, AND THE MULTI-MODEL CONSENSUS, DEVIATING
BELOW THE CONSENSUS AFTER TAU 72 DUE TO THE OUTLIER OF HAFS-A.
