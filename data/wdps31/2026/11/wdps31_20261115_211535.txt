SATELLITE ANALYSIS, INITIAL POSITION AND INTENSITY DISCUSSION:
ANIMATED MULTISPECTRAL SATELLITE IMAGERY (MSI) DEPICTS A WELL-
DEFINED, PARTIALLY EXPOSED, AND HIGHLY ASYMMETRIC LOW-LEVEL 
CIRCULATION CENTER (LLCC), WITH PERSISTENT DEEP CONVECTION CONFINED 
TO THE SOUTHERN SEMICIRCLE OF THE CIRCULATION. THE NORTHEASTERN 
QUADRANT OF THE CIRCULATION IS DEVOID OF SIGNIFICANT CONVECTIVE 
ACTIVITY, LIKELY DUE TO MID-TROPOSPHERIC DRY AIR ENTRAINMENT, AS 
INDICATED IN HIGH-RESOLUTION MODEL-DERIVED CROSS-SECTIONS AND CIRA 
ADVECTED LAYER PRECIPITABLE WATER (ALPW) IMAGERY. A 151558Z SSMIS 37 
GHZ MICROWAVE IMAGE REVEALED A NASCENT MICROWAVE EYE FEATURE, WITH 
DEEP CONVECTIVE BANDING DEVELOPING ALONG THE SOUTHERN FLANK AND 
SHALLOW BANDING FEATURES TO THE NORTHEAST. IN THE UPPER-LEVELS, A 
WEAK 200 MB POINT-SOURCE ANTICYCLONE IS ANALYZED NEAR TC 04P, WHILE 
AN EXPANSIVE 200 MB ANTICYCLONE IS POSITIONED TO THE SOUTHEAST, 
PROVIDING SUBSTANTIAL DIVERGENT POLEWARD OUTFLOW OVER TC 04P. THE 
INITIAL POSITION IS ASSESSED WITH HIGH CONFIDENCE BASED ON THE 
EXPOSED LLCC IN THE MSI AND EXTRAPOLATION OF THE MICROWAVE EYE 
FEATURE. THE INITIAL INTENSITY IS ASSESSED WITH HIGH CONFIDENCE BASED 
ON A 151630Z SMOS PASS, WHICH REVEALED AN ASYMMETRIC SURFACE WIND 
FIELD WITH GALE-FORCE WINDS OF UP TO 40 KNOTS ALONG THE SOUTHERN 
FLANK OF THE CIRCULATION. ENVIRONMENTAL CONDITIONS ARE FAVORABLE, 
CHARACTERIZED BY LOW TO MODERATE DEEP-LAYER VERTICAL WIND SHEAR 
(VWS), HIGH SEA SURFACE TEMPERATURES (SSTS), HIGH OCEAN HEAT CONTENT 
(OHC), AND STRONG POLEWARD OUTFLOW ALOFT.

INITIAL WIND RADII BASIS: SMOS DATA 

CURRENT STEERING MECHANISM: NORTHERN FLANK OF A LOW- TO MID-LEVEL
SUBTROPICAL RIDGE (STR) CENTERED FAR TO THE SOUTHEAST.

3. FORECAST REASONING.

SIGNIFICANT FORECAST CHANGES: THERE ARE NO SIGNIFICANT CHANGES TO
THE FORECAST FROM THE PREVIOUS WARNING.

FORECAST DISCUSSION: TROPICAL CYCLONE (TC) 04P HAS BEGUN TRANSLATING 
SLOWLY WESTWARD AS THE DEEP-LAYER STR TO THE SOUTHEAST BUILDS 
WESTWARD POLEWARD OF THE SYSTEM. A SLIGHT TURN WEST-SOUTHWESTWARD IS 
FORECAST TO BEGIN IMMINENTLY AND CONTINUE THROUGH TAU 36 AS THE 
WESTERN EXTENSION OF THE RIDGE REACHES ITS MAXIMUM AMPLITUDE AND 
ZONAL EXTENT. BY TAU 36, THE STR WILL BEGIN RETROGRADING EASTWARD, 
PLACING TC 04P IN A RAPIDLY WEAKENING STEERING PATTERN WITHIN A 
DEVELOPING COL BETWEEN THE STR TO THE SOUTHEAST AND A SECONDARY STR 
FAR TO THE WEST NEAR THE SOLOMON ISLANDS. FROM TAU 48 TO TAU 72, TC 
04P WILL DRIFT POLEWARD AT JUST 2-3 KNOTS, QUASI-STATIONARY WITHIN 
THE COL REGION. AFTER TAU 72, AS THE SYSTEM RAPIDLY WEAKENS AND 
VERTICALLY DECOUPLES, IT WILL COME UNDER THE STEERING INFLUENCE OF 
THE LOWER-LEVEL EASTERLY TRADE-WIND FLOW ALONG THE NORTHERN PERIPHERY 
OF THE STR TO THE SOUTH, WITH THE SHALLOW REMNANT VORTEX RAPIDLY 
SHIFTING BACK TO THE WEST BY THE END OF THE FORECAST PERIOD. 
REGARDING INTENSITY, TC 04P IS POSITIONED IN A FAVORABLE ENVIRONMENT, 
CURRENTLY INHIBITED SOLELY BY THE ASYMMETRIC NATURE OF THE 
CIRCULATION AND MODERATE VERTICAL VORTEX TILT. HIGH-RESOLUTION 
DYNAMICAL MODELING SUGGESTS VERTICAL VORTEX ALIGNMENT AND CONVECTIVE 
SYMMETRIZATION WILL BE ACHIEVED WITHIN THE NEXT 12 HOURS, 
FACILITATING SUBSEQUENT RAPID INTENSIFICATION (RI) AFTER TAU 24. THE 
SYSTEM IS FORECAST TO REACH A PEAK INTENSITY OF 90 KNOTS BY TAU 48. 
AFTER TAU 60, RAPIDLY INCREASING MID-LATITUDE WESTERLY WIND SHEAR AND 
PERVASIVE DRY AIR ENTRAINMENT WILL BEGIN TO ERODE THE SYSTEM. AS TC 
04P DECELERATES AND TRACKS OVER LOW OHC WATERS, SIGNIFICANT INDUCED 
OCEANIC UPWELLING IS EXPECTED, WHICH WILL ACCELERATE WEAKENING AFTER 
TAU 72. THERE IS SIGNIFICANT BIFURCATION IN THE EXTENDED-RANGE 
FORECAST, WITH SOME MODELS, PARTICULARLY THE ENSEMBLES AND DYNAMICAL 
PHYSICS-BASED MODELS, INDICATING A CONTINUOUS WEST-SOUTHWESTWARD 
TRACK THROUGH THE ENTIRE FORECAST PERIOD. THE PRIMARY MECHANISM 
DRIVING THIS DISCREPANCY IS THAT THE DYNAMICAL MODELS DO NOT 
INTENSIFY THE SYSTEM AS AGGRESSIVELY AS THE DATA-DRIVEN AI MODELS, 
RESULTING IN A SHALLOWER VORTEX GOVERNED BY A LOWER-LEVEL, MORE 
ZONALLY WESTWARD STEERING FLOW. CONSEQUENTLY, AN ALTERNATE SCENARIO 
EXISTS WHEREIN A WEAKER, SHALLOWER SYSTEM MAINTAINS A GENERAL WEST-
SOUTHWESTWARD TRAJECTORY AT A FASTER TRANSLATIONAL PACE THAN DEPICTED 
IN THE OFFICIAL JTWC FORECAST.    

MODEL DISCUSSION: DETERMINISTIC AND ENSEMBLE TRACK GUIDANCE IS IN 
RELATIVELY GOOD AGREEMENT THROUGH TAU 48, BUT RAPIDLY DIVERGES BEYOND 
THAT POINT. AT TAU 48, CROSS-TRACK SPREAD IS JUST UNDER 100 NM, 
BOUNDED BY THE EC-AIFS ON THE SOUTHERN EDGE OF THE GUIDANCE ENVELOPE 
AND THE AI-ECEPS ON THE NORTHERN EDGE. BY TAU 72, CROSS-TRACK SPREAD 
INCREASES TO 150 NM, WITH THE GEFS BECOMING THE WESTWARD OUTLIER 
WHILE THE EC-AIFS REMAINS THE EASTERN OUTLIER. BY TAU 120, CROSS-
TRACK UNCERTAINTY EXPANDS TO 350 NM BETWEEN THE GEFS, WHICH ADVECTS 
THE SYSTEM WESTWARD TO 168W, AND THE EC-AIFS, WHICH DEFLECTS THE 
SYSTEM SOUTHWARD NEAR 163W. THE GFS, ECEPS MEAN, AND AI-ECEPS MEAN 
GENERALLY FOLLOW THE GEFS TRAJECTORY, BUT AT A SLOWER TRANSLATIONAL 
SPEED. THE REMAINDER OF THE AI MODELS, INCLUDING THE GOOGLE DEEPMIND 
(GDMI) AND THE AI CONSENSUS (AICN), ALONG WITH THE ECMWF 
DETERMINISTIC TRACKER, ARE TIGHTLY CLUSTERED ROUGHLY MIDWAY BETWEEN 
THE AFOREMENTIONED OUTLIERS. ENSEMBLE SUITES ARE GENERALLY IN 
AGREEMENT THAT THE SYSTEM WILL DECELERATE SIGNIFICANTLY BY TAU 48, 
BUT EXHIBIT SUBSTANTIAL INTRA-ENSEMBLE DISPERSION THEREAFTER 
REGARDING THE SUBSEQUENT TRACK DIRECTION. THE OFFICIAL JTWC FORECAST 
IS PLACED NEAR THE GDMI-AICN-ECMWF CLUSTER WITH MEDIUM TO LOW 
CONFIDENCE. INTENSITY GUIDANCE IS BIFURCATED, WITH THE DYNAMICAL 
PHYSICS MODELS (HWRF, HAFS-A, GFS, COAMPS-TC [COTC], AND CTCX) 
DEPICTING MODERATE INTENSIFICATION TO BETWEEN 50 AND 75 KNOTS. 
CONVERSELY, THE GDMI, ECMWF, AND MULTIPLE RI AIDS (RIPA, RIDE, AND 
FRIA) DEPICT A MORE RAPID RATE OF INTENSIFICATION, REACHING A PEAK 
BETWEEN 90 AND 110 KNOTS BY TAU 48 BEFORE RAPIDLY WEAKENING THROUGH 
THE REMAINDER OF THE FORECAST PERIOD. THE JTWC INTENSITY FORECAST IS 
POSITIONED NEAR THE ECMWF AND GDMI SOLUTIONS THROUGH THE FORECAST 
PERIOD WITH MEDIUM TO LOW CONFIDENCE.
