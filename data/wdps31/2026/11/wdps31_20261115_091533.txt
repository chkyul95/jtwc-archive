SATELLITE ANALYSIS, INITIAL POSITION AND INTENSITY DISCUSSION:
ANIMATED ENHANCED INFRARED (EIR) SATELLITE IMAGERY DEPICTS TROPICAL
CYCLONE (TC) 04P AS A SLIGHTLY ELONGATED CIRCULATION COMPRISED OF
DEEP CONVECTIVE BANDING EXPANDING OFF OF THE NORTHERN AND SOUTHERN
FLANKS. A  150548Z F-16 PASS REVEALED A DEVELOPING LOW LEVEL
CIRCULATION CENTER (LLCC) VERTICALLY ALIGNED BETWEEN THE 37 GHZ AND
91 GHZ IMAGES WITH THE MOST ROBUST CONVECTION LOCATED WITHIN THE
EASTERN BAND. ENVIRONMENTAL CONDITIONS ARE ASSESSED AS HIGHLY
FAVORABLE WITH HIGH (29-30 C) SEA SURFACE TEMPERATURES, LOW (5-10
KTS) VERTICAL WIND SHEAR (VWS), AND ROBUST RADIAL OUTFLOW ALOFT DUE
TO A POINT SOURCE POSITIONED JUST TO THE NORTHWEST OF THE SYSTEM.
THE INITIAL POSITION IS PLACED WITH HIGH CONFIDENCE BASED ON THE
SSMIS MICROWAVE DATA. THE INITIAL INTENSITY IS ASSESSED WITH HIGH
CONFIDENCE BASED ON 2.5 DVORAK FIXES FROM PGTW AND NFFN AS WELL AS
THE IMPROVED CONVECTIVE STRUCTURE. THE INITIAL WIND RADII ARE
ASSESSED WITH LOW CONFIDENCE DUE TO A LACK OF MORE RECENT WIND
SPEED DATA.

3. FORECAST REASONING.

SIGNIFICANT FORECAST CHANGES: THIS INITIAL PROGNOSTIC REASONING
MESSAGE ESTABLISHES THE FORECAST PHILOSOPHY.

FORECAST DISCUSSION: TC 04B IS FORECAST TO TRACK GENERALLY WEST TO
WEST-SOUTHWESTWARD OUT TO TAU 60 AS THE DEVELOPING SYSTEM IS
STEERED PRIMARILY BY THE LOW TO MID-LEVEL SUBTROPICAL RIDGE (STR)
CENTERED TO THE SOUTH. AFTER TAU 60, A SLOW IN TRACK SPEED AND
POLEWARD TURN IS FORECAST AS THE DEEP-LAYER STR CENTERED TO THE
EAST-SOUTHEAST BUILDS A WESTWARD EXTENSION AND 04B ENTERS A COL REGION
BETWEEN THIS STR AND ANOTHER, WEAKER STR CENTERED TO THE WEST. NEAR
THE END OF THE FORECAST PERIOD, AN INCOMING MID-LATITUDE TROUGH IS
EXPECTED TO REORIENT AND WEAKEN THE EASTERN STR, CAUSING A SLIGHT
WESTWARD TURN BY TAU 96. THE OVERALL STEERING ENVIRONMENT
THROUGHOUT THE FORECAST PERIOD IS EXPECTED TO REMAIN RELATIVELY
WEAK AND HIGHLY DEPENDENT ON THE INTENSITY OF 04B AT THAT TIME.
REGARDING INTENSITY, 04B IS EXPECTED TO INTENSIFY JUST BELOW THE
RATE OF EXPLICIT RAPID INTENSIFICATION (RI) OUT TO TAU 36 AS
OUTFLOW ALOFT REMAINS HIGHLY CONDUCIVE AND ALL OTHER ENVIRONMENTAL
CONDITIONS REMAIN FAVORABLE. A PEAK OF 80 KTS IS FORECAST BETWEEN TAUS 
48 AND 60, FOLLOWED BY GENERAL WEAKENING THEREAFTER AS MID-LAYER VWS 
INCREASES AND BEGINS TO ENTRAIN DRY AIR INTO THE CORE. THE PEAK 
INTENSITY AND ASSOCIATED TIMING OF 04B WILL HIGHLY IMPACT ITS TRACK, 
AS THE SYSTEM GAINS INTENSITY IT IS EXPECTED TO BE STEERED PRIMARILY 
BY THE AFOREMENTIONED DEEP-LAYER FEATURES, WHEREAS IF IT PEAKS LOWER 
IT WILL REMAIN STEERED BY THE LOW-TO-MID LEVEL FLOW.

MODEL DISCUSSION: TRACK MODEL GUIDANCE IS IN GENERALLY GOOD
AGREEMENT ON AN OVERALL WEST-SOUTHWESTWARD TRACK FOR THE DURATION
OF THE FORECAST PERIOD, HOWEVER BY TAU 60 MODELS SPLIT TO DEPICT
EITHER A STRAIGHT RUNNING AND STEADY TRACK SPEED (GFS, ECMWF,
NAVGEM, GEFS, EPS) OR A POLEWARD TURN (GOOGLE DEEPMIND, HAFS-A,
AIGFS, AIFS). HOWEVER, EVEN THE LATTER GROUPING IS SPLIT, WITH
SEVERAL DEPICTING A WESTWARD TURN AT OR AFTER TAU 120 WHILE OTHERS
RECURVE 04B SOUTHEASTWARD. NAVGEM IS THE FASTEST OUTLIER, BRINGING
THE SYSTEM CLOSE TO AMERICAN SAMOA BY TAU 120 WHILE UKMET AND UKMET
ENSEMBLE ARE THE ONLY MODELS TO DEPICT AN EQUATORWARD TURN AND
SUBSEQUENT NORTHWEST TRACK AT TAU 72. ENSEMBLE MODEL ANALYSIS
REVEALS FURTHER SPREAD, WITH THE PHYSICS-BASED MODELS FAVORING THE
SLIGHTLY FASTER STRAIGHT-RUNNING TRACK, WHILE SI-BASED MODELS SLOW
THE SYSTEM AFTER TAU 60 AND SHOW LARGER SPREAD WITH MEMBERS
DEPICTING STRAIGHT-RUNNING AS WELL AS RECURVING SCENARIOS. OVERALL,
THE JTWC FORECAST IS PLACED WITH MEDIUM CONFIDENCE OUT TO TAU 72,
IN GOOD AGREEMENT WITH THE MULTI-MODEL (EXCLUDING NAVGEM) AND SI
CONSENSUS AND LOW CONFIDENCE THEREAFTER, FAVORING THE SI CONSENSUS
IN A SLOWER AND MORE POLEWARD TRACK. THERE REMAINS A POSSIBILITY FOR 
THE STRAIGHT RUNNING SCENARIO TO OCCUR, IF THE SYSTEM DOES NOT 
INTENSIFY AS FORECAST. MODEL INTENSITY GUIDANCE IS ALSO SPREAD, WITH A 
MULTITUDE OF RI AIDS POPULATING, AS WELL AS GOOGLE DEEPMIND DEPICTING 
RI AND A PEAK OF 100 KTS AT TAU 48. GFS, HAFS-A, AND COAMPS-TC MAKE UP 
THE LOW END OF THE GUIDANCE PACKAGE, FORECASTING A PEAK OF 65 KTS 
BETWEEN TAUS 24 AND 60. ALL MODELS AGREE ON STEADY WEAKENING AFTER TAU 
60 WITH THE EXCEPTION OF HAFS. THE OFFICIAL JTWC INTENSITY FORECAST IS 
ASSESSED WITH MEDIUM CONFIDENCE OUT TO TAU 72 BASED ON THE HIGH AMOUNT 
OF RI AIDS THAT HAVE POPULATED AND THE FAVORABLE CONDITIONS. THE 
FORECAST IS ASSESSED WITH LOW CONFIDENCE THEREAFTER AS THE GUIDANCE 
PACKAGE IS SPREAD BY 45 KTS AT TAU 120. THE FORECAST IS IN LINE WITH 
THE TREND OF GOOGLE DEEPMIND AS THE FORECAST TRACK ALIGNS WITH ITS 
FORECAST TRACK, BUT LOWER THAN ITS EXPECTED PEAK INTENSITY DUE TO
UNCERTAINTY REGARDING THE TIMING AND DURATION OF RI.
