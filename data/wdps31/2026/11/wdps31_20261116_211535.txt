TROPICAL CYCLONE (TC) 04P IS UNDERGOING EXPLOSIVE INTENSIFICATION.
THE RAPIDLY CONSOLIDATING CENTRAL DENSE OVERCAST (CDO) CONTAINS
VERY COLD CLOUD TOPS AS COLD AS -91 DEGREES CELSIUS. ANIMATED

EMERGING WITHIN THE CDO, WITH VORTICAL HOT TOWERS ROTATING AROUND

WELL-DEVELOPED MICROWAVE EYE FEATURE AND EXTENSIVE CONVECTIVE
BANDING. TC 04P IS TRAVERSING A FAVORABLE ENVIRONMENT OF VERY WARM
SEA SURFACE TEMPERATURES (SST) NEAR 30 DEGREES CELSIUS, MODERATE
DIVERGENCE ALOFT, RICH DEEP-LAYER MOISTURE, AND MODERATE VERTICAL
WIND SHEAR (VWS). THE INITIAL POSITION IS PLACED WITH HIGH
CONFIDENCE BASED ON THE NASCENT, RAGGED EYE IN MSI. THE INITIAL
INTENSITY HAS BEEN RAISED TO 85 KTS WITH MEDIUM CONFIDENCE BASED ON
THE RAPIDLY IMPROVED SATELLITE PRESENTATION. IT SHOULD BE NOTED
THAT THE DVORAK EIR EYE TECHNIQUE YIELDED INSTANTANEOUS DATA
T-NUMBERS OF 5.5-6.0. THE ASSESSED INTENSITY IS SET HIGHER THAN THE
AGENCY DVORAK CLASSIFICATIONS, WHICH LIKELY ARE ATTEMPTING TO CATCH

3. FORECAST REASONING.

SIGNIFICANT FORECAST CHANGES: THE FORECAST PEAK INTENSITY HAS BEEN
INCREASED BY 25 KTS.

FORECAST DISCUSSION: TC 04P IS TRANSITING WESTWARD TO
WEST-SOUTHWESTWARD ALONG THE NORTHWESTERN PERIPHERY OF A DEEP-LAYER
STR LOCATED TO THE SOUTHEAST. A DEEP MID-LEVEL TROUGH PASSING
SOUTHEAST OF TC 04P WILL ERODE THE EASTERN PORTION OF THE STR,
PRODUCING A SUBSTANTIAL WEAKNESS IN THE STEERING PATTERN. AS A
RESULT, TC 04P WILL TURN POLEWARD DURING THE NEXT 24 HOURS WHILE
GRADUALLY SLOWING. THE FAVORABLE ENVIRONMENT WILL PERSIST THROUGH
TAU 24, ALLOWING RAPID INTENSIFICATION (RI) TO CONTINUE. AFTER TAU
24, INCREASING VWS SHOULD INITIATE A WEAKENING TREND. COMPARED TO
THE PREVIOUS FORECAST, THE CURRENT FORECAST SHOWS A STRONGER
POLEWARD MOTION FOLLOWED BY A BRIEF SOUTHEASTWARD RECURVATURE
THROUGH TAU 72. THE STRONGER AND DEEPER VORTEX WILL RESPOND MORE
STRONGLY TO THE ERODING DEEP-LAYER STR AND THE MID-LEVEL TROUGH
THAN IN THE PREVIOUS FORECAST. AFTER TAU 48, STRONGER VWS, LOWER
OCEAN HEAT CONTENT (OHC), AND THE POTENTIAL FOR COOL-WATER
UPWELLING, PARTICULARLY AS THE SYSTEM SLOWS, SHOULD LEAD TO MORE
RAPID WEAKENING. FROM TAU 96 TO TAU 120, AS TC 04P WEAKENS AND
BECOMES MORE SHALLOW, A LOWER-LEVEL RIDGE BUILDING TO THE SOUTH
WILL STEER THE SYSTEM SHARPLY WESTWARD.

MODEL DISCUSSION: SIGNIFICANT TRACK SPREAD EXISTS AMONG THE
GUIDANCE, PRIMARILY OWING TO DIFFERENCES IN THE PREDICTED VORTEX
INTENSITY AND THE EVOLUTION OF THE RIDGES AND TROUGHS POLEWARD OF
THE SYSTEM. THE WEAKER SOLUTIONS, INCLUDING GFS AND ECMWF, DEPICT A
SHORTER POLEWARD TURN FOLLOWED BY A DEFLECTION BACK TOWARD THE
WEST. THE STRONGER SOLUTIONS, INCLUDING GOOGLE DEEPMIND AND HAFS-A,
DEPICT GREATER POLEWARD AND SOUTHEASTWARD MOTION. THE JTWC FORECAST
TRACK LIES NEAR THE AI-MODEL MEAN (AICN) BUT IS LESS AGGRESSIVE
WITH THE SOUTHWARD AND SOUTHEASTWARD MOTION THAN THE GOOGLE
DEEPMIND AND HAFS-A SOLUTIONS. INTENSITY GUIDANCE ALSO EXHIBITS
APPRECIABLE SPREAD. GIVEN THE OBSERVED AND ONGOING RI OF TC 04P,
THE JTWC INTENSITY FORECAST IS PLACED ON THE HIGHER END OF THE
GUIDANCE ENVELOPE, ABOVE THE 110-115 KTS INDICATED BY DEEPMIND BUT
BELOW THE 140 KTS INDICATED BY HAFS-A. ROUGHLY 35 PERCENT OF THE
DEEPMIND ENSEMBLE MEMBERS ACHIEVE AT LEAST 130 KTS BY TAU 24, WHILE
90 PERCENT REACH 100 KTS. SUBSEQUENT FORECASTS MAY REQUIRE A MORE
AGGRESSIVE INTENSIFICATION FORECAST. AFTER TAU 24, THE JTWC
INTENSITY FORECAST GRADUALLY CONVERGES TOWARD THE DEEPMIND
SOLUTION.
