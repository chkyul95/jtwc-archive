SATELLITE ANALYSIS, INITIAL POSITION AND INTENSITY DISCUSSION:
ANIMATED ENHANCED INFRARED (EIR) SATELLITE IMAGERY DEPICTS TROPICAL
CYCLONE (TC) 04P (XAVIER) WITH A MOSTLY EXPOSED LOW-LEVEL
CIRCULATION CENTER (LLCC) UP UNTIL 210300Z, BUT THEN BECAME
OBSCURED BY DEEP CONVECTION DEVELOPING ALONG THE SOUTHERN
SEMICIRCLE. A 210508Z F17 SSMIS 91 GHZ MICROWAVE IMAGE REVEALED
FRAGMENTED CONVECTIVE BANDING ISOLATED TO THE SOUTHEASTERN QUADRANT
AND LOW-LEVEL CLOUD LINES WRAPPING INTO THE CENTER. THE INITIAL
POSITION IS PLACED WITH HIGH CONFIDENCE BASED ON THE TIMELY SSMIS
IMAGE. A LATE ARRIVING 210527Z SENTINEL-1C SAR PASS MEASURED A VMAX
OF RIGHT AROUND 50 KTS WITHIN A SMALL AREA ABOUT 25 NM SOUTH OF THE
CENTER. TAKING INTO ACCOUNT THE SAR DATA, THE SYSTEM IS LIKELY
CLOSER TO 45 KTS 1-MIN INTENSITY. WHEN COMPARING THE WIND FIELD
REPRESENTED IN THE EARLIER ASCAT PASS TO THE MORE RECENT SAR DATA,
THE SYSTEM HAS SLIGHTLY INTENSIFIED OVER THE PAST 6 HOURS. HOWEVER,
THE SAR PASS ALSO REVEALED THAT THE WINDS WITHIN THE NORTHERN
SEMICIRCLE ARE RELATIVELY QUITE WEAK. IN THE NORTHWESTERN QUADRANT
WINDS WERE MEASURED TO BE 25-30 KTS, SHOWING THE ASYMMETRY IN THE
STRUCTURE OF 04P. ENVIRONMENTAL ANALYSIS INDICATES THAT 04P REMAINS
PLACED WITHIN AN UNFAVORABLE ENVIRONMENT CHARACTERIZED BY MODERATE
POLEWARD OUTFLOW ALOFT, MODERATE (15-20 KTS) WESTERLY VERTICAL WIND
SHEAR, AND DRY AIR ENTRAINMENT.

INITIAL WIND RADII BASIS: PERSISTENCE WITH SLIGHT ADJUSTMENTS MADE

3. FORECAST REASONING.

SIGNIFICANT FORECAST CHANGES: THERE ARE NO SIGNIFICANT CHANGES TO
THE FORECAST FROM THE PREVIOUS WARNING.

FORECAST DISCUSSION: TC 04P IS FORECAST TO TRACK
WEST-SOUTHWESTWARD, ALONG THE NORTHWESTERN PERIPHERY OF THE
SUBTROPICAL RIDGE TO THE SOUTHEAST THROUGH TAU 48. THE SYSTEM IS
EXPECTED TO REMAIN NORTH OF AMERICAN SAMOA, THOUGH THERE STILL
REMAINS SOME UNCERTAINTY IN HOW CLOSE IT WILL ULTIMATELY GET TO THE
ISLAND. AROUND TAU 48, A SHORTWAVE TROUGH WILL BREAK DOWN THE
CURRENT STEERING RIDGE AND A SEPARATE RIDGE WILL BUILD IN BEHIND
IT, NORTH OF NEW ZEALAND. THE FORMATION OF THIS NEW RIDGE IS
EXPECTED TO DEFLECT 04P NORTHWESTWARD. THE NORTHWESTWARD TRAJECTORY
WILL THEN PERSIST THROUGH THE REMAINDER OF THE FORECAST PERIOD (TAU
72). IN TERMS OF INTENSITY, 04P IS FORECAST TO MAINTAIN ITS CURRENT
INTENSITY THROUGH TAU 36 DUE PRIMARILY TO INTERMITTENT CONVECTIVE
DEVELOPMENT DEPICTED IN MESOSCALE MODELS AND WARM SEA SURFACE
TEMPERATURES. IF THE CONVECTION FAILS TO FORM IN THE FACE OF
PERSISTENT SHEAR AND DRY AIR, A QUICKER WEAKENING TREND WOULD ENSUE
THAN IS CURRENTLY DEPICTED IN THE JTWC FORECAST. AFTER TAU 36, DRY
AIR ENTRAINMENT INTO THE CORE WILL BECOME SIGNIFICANT, FURTHER
WEAKENING THE VORTEX. AS A RESULT, 04P IS FORECAST TO DISSIPATE
OVER WATER NO LATER THAN TAU 72. 

MODEL DISCUSSION: TRACK GUIDANCE IS IN MODERATE AGREEMENT
THROUGHOUT THE FORECAST PERIOD WITH THE CROSS-TRACK SPREAD MEASURED
TO BE ABOUT 110 NM AT TAU 48 AND 160 NM AT TAU 72. THE EXPERIMENTAL
GOOGLE DEEPMIND WNV-3 TRACKER IS THE FURTHEST EQUATORWARD MEMBER
WHILE NAVGEM IS THE FURTHERST POLEWARD. THE JTWC TRACK FORECAST IS
HEDGED SLIGHTLY EQUATORWARD OF THE MULTI-MODEL CONSENSUS WITH
MEDIUM CONFIDENCE. INTENSITY GUIDANCE IS SLIGHTLY SPLIT IN THE NEAR
TERM, WITH HAFS-A AND COAMPS-TC SHOWING SLIGHT INTENSIFICATION
THROUGH TAU 24 WHILE THE GOOGLE DEEPMIND AND GFS SHOW WEAKENING
STARTING FROM TAU 0. ALL MODELS THEN AGREE ON WEAKENING FROM TAU 36
ONWARD. THE JTWC INTENSITY FORECAST IS PLACED NEAR THE CONSENSUS
WITH MEDIUM CONFIDENCE.
