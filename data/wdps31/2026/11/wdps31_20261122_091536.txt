3. FORECAST REASONING.

SIGNIFICANT FORECAST CHANGES: THERE ARE NO SIGNIFICANT CHANGES TO
THE FORECAST FROM THE PREVIOUS WARNING.

FORECAST DISCUSSION: TC 04P MADE AN UNEXPECTED NORTHWESTWARD JOG
OVER THE PAST 6 HOURS AS THE VORTEX BECAME EXTREMELY SHALLOW,
FOLLOWING THE LOW-LEVEL STEERING COMPRISED OF THE EASTERLY TRADE
WINDS. THIS HAS LED TO AN INCREASE IN THE CPA RELATIVE TO AMERICAN
SAMOA. THE SYSTEM IS NOW FORECAST TO TRACK JUST SOUTH OF WEST
THROUGH TAU 24, UNDER THE INFLUENCE OF THE STR TO THE SOUTHEAST.
NEAR TAU 24, THE STR WILL PROPAGATE EAST DUE TO AN INCOMING
SHORTWAVE TROUGH AND A SEPARATE STR WILL BUILD NORTH OF NEW
ZEALAND, REDIRECTING 04P WEST-NORTHWESTWARD THROUGH THE REMAINDER
OF THE FORECAST PERIOD (TAU 60). REGARDING INTENSITY, 04P IS
FORECAST TO GRADUALLY WEAKEN THROUGHOUT THE FORECAST PERIOD IN
RESPONSE TO PERSISTENT SHEAR AND DRY AIR ENTRAINMENT. THE PERIODIC
DEVELOPMENT OF CONVECTION COULD ALLOW FOR A SINUSOIDAL INTENSITY
TREND OVER THE NEXT 24 HOURS. FOLLOWING TAU 24, DRY AIR INTRUSION
WILL WORSEN, CAUSING 04P TO DISSIPATE OVER WATER NEAR TAU 48, NORTH
OF SAMOA. 

MODEL DISCUSSION: MODEL TRACK GUIDANCE CONTINUES TO BECOME MORE
ALIGNED, WITH THE EXCEPTION OF NAVGEM, WHICH IS SIGNIFICANTLY
FURTHER TO THE SOUTH THAN THE REMAINDER OF GUIDANCE. DISCOUNTING
NAVGEM, THE CROSS-TRACK SPREAD AT TAU 48 IS MEASURED TO BE ABOUT 60
NM. AS A RESULT, THE JTWC TRACK FORECAST IS PLACED NEAR THE
MODIFIED CONSENSUS (REMOVING NAVGEM) WITH HIGH CONFIDENCE.
INTENSITY GUIDANCE GENERALLY AGREES ON WEAKENING FROM TAU 12 ONWARD
WITH NO SIGNIFICANT OUTLIERS PRESENT. THE JTWC INTENSITY FORECAST
IS PLACED SLIGHTLY BELOW THE CONSENSUS THROUGHOUT THE FORECAST
PERIOD WITH MEDIUM CONFIDENCE.
