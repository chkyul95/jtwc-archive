C). THE INITIAL POSITION IS PLACED WITH HIGH CONFIDENCE BASED ON

3. FORECAST REASONING.

SIGNIFICANT FORECAST CHANGES: THERE ARE NO SIGNIFICANT CHANGES TO
THE FORECAST FROM THE PREVIOUS WARNING.

FORECAST DISCUSSION: TC 04P IS FORECAST TO TRACK SLOWLY
SOUTH-SOUTHEASTWARD AS IT ATTEMPTS TO ROUND THE STR AXIS OVER THE
NEXT 24 HOURS. HOWEVER, A SHORTWAVE TROUGH WILL BREAK DOWN THE
RIDGE, LEAVING 04P PLACED WITHIN A WEAK STEERING ENVIRONMENT WITH
NO DEFINED STEERING MECHANISM FROM TAU 24 THROUGH TAU 48. AROUND
TAU 60, THE VORTEX IS EXPECTED TO BECOME CONSIDERABLY MORE SHALLOW,
ALLOWING FOR THE STEERING PATTERN TO BE DOMINATED BY A LOW TO
MID-LEVEL STR SITUATED TO THE SOUTHEAST. THIS WILL CAUSE 04P TO
PICK UP SPEED IN THE WEST-SOUTHWESTWARD DIRECTION THROUGH TAU 96 AS
THE SYSTEM TRAVERSES THE NORTHERN PERIPHERY OF THE RIDGE. FOLLOWING
TAU 96, 04P IS FORECAST TO DIP MORE SOUTHWARD AS THE RIDGE
PROPAGATES EASTWARD AND THE SYSTEM ROUNDS THE WESTERN EXTENT OF IT.
IN TERMS OF INTENSITY, 04P IS FORECAST TO CONTINUE WEAKENING
THROUGH TAU 24 DUE TO PERSISTENT DRY AIR AND MODERATE SHEAR ACTING
ON THE VORTEX. A MORE RAPID RATE OF WEAKENING IS FORECAST TO OCCUR
FROM TAU 24 TO TAU 48 DUE TO THE EXTREMELY SLOW MOTION OF THE STORM
CAUSING THE UPWELLING OF COOLER WATERS. 04P WILL THEN FURTHER
WEAKEN AS IT TRACKS WESTWARD DUE TO SIGNIFICANT DRY AIR ENTRAINMENT
AND CONTINUED WESTERLY SHEAR.  

MODEL DISCUSSION: TRACK GUIDANCE IS IN RELATIVELY GOOD AGREEMENT
THROUGH TAU 72 WITH THE EXCEPTION OF NAVGEM, WHICH TRACKS THE
VORTEX STRAIGHT SOUTHWARD INTO THE RIDGE. DISCOUNTING NAVGEM, THE
CROSS-TRACK SPREAD AT TAU 72 IS MEASURED TO BE ABOUT 90 NM WHILE
THE ALONG-TRACK SPREAD IS 150 NM. THE FASTEST MODEL IS GALWEM WHILE
THE SLOWEST IS ECMWF. BY TAU 120, CROSS-TRACK SPREAD INCREASES TO
200 NM WHILE ALONG-TRACK SPREAD IS OVER 300 NM. THE GOOGLE DEEPMIND
AND ECMWF ENSEMBLE MEANS TRACK THE VORTEX EAST OF AMERICAN SAMOA
WHILE GALWEM AND JGSM ARE TO THE WEST OF AMERICAN SAMOA. THE GFS
AND GEFS MEAN ARE IN THE MIDDLE AND TRACK THE SYSTEM VERY CLOSE TO
THE ISLAND AT TAU 120. THE JTWC TRACK FORECAST IS PLACED CLOSE TO
THE MODIFIED CONSENSUS (REMOVING NAVGEM) THROUGH TAU 72, AND THEN
SLIGHTLY TO THE EAST THROUGH TAU 120. INTENSITY GUIDANCE AGREES
ACROSS THE BOARD ON WEAKENING THROUGHOUT THE FORECAST PERIOD.
HAFS-A IS THE QUICKEST TO WEAKEN THE SYSTEM, SUGGESTING AN INTENSITY
BELOW TC STRENGTH NEAR TAU 72. THE JTWC INTENSITY FORECAST IS SET
CLOSE TO THE CONSENSUS WITH MEDIUM CONFIDENCE.
