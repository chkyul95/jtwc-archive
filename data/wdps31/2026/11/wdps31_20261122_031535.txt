3. FORECAST REASONING.

SIGNIFICANT FORECAST CHANGES: SHIFTED TRACK NORTH DUE TO THE
WESTWARD ACCELERATED TRACK MOTION OVER THE PAST SIX HOURS  

FORECAST DISCUSSION: TROPICAL CYCLONE (TC) 04P IS FORECAST TO TURN
AND TRACK WEST-SOUTHWESTWARD THROUGH TAU 36 AS IT TRACKS ALONG THE
NORTHWEST PERIPHERY OF THE LOW- TO MID-LEVEL SUBTROPICAL RIDGE. THE
SYSTEM SHOULD MAINTAIN AN INTENSITY RANGING FROM 40-45 KNOTS
THROUGH TAU 24 ALTHOUGH A FASTER WEAKENING TREND IS POSSIBLE DUE TO
THE UNFAVORABLE ENVIRONMENT CHARACTERIZED BY EXTENSIVE DRY AIR AND
MODERATE TO HIGH VERTICAL WIND SHEAR (VWS). AFTER TAU 36, THE
SYSTEM IS EXPECTED TO TURN SHARPLY WEST-NORTHWESTWARD ALONG THE
NORTHERN PERIPHERY OF A STRONG HIGH FORECAST TO BUILD TO THE SOUTH.
TC 04P WILL LIKELY MAINTAIN EXTENSIVE GALE-FORCE WINDS OVER THE
SOUTHERN SEMICIRCLE DUE TO THE STRONG PRESSURE GRADIENT ASSOCIATED WITH 
THE HIGH, WHICH WILL CONTINUE TO IMPACT AMERICAN SAMOA THROUGH THE 
FORECAST PERIOD.

MODEL DISCUSSION: IN GENERAL, THE AI-BASED MODELS HAVE MAINTAINED A 
MORE CONSISTENT TRACK FORECAST WITH A SLIGHT DIP THROUGH TAU 36 THEN A 
SHARP WEST-NORTHWESTWARD TURN THROUGH TAU 60. THE PHYSICS-BASED MODELS 
HAVE SHIFTED INTO CLOSER ALIGNMENT WITH THE AI-BASED MODELS SOLUTIONS 
WITH THE EXCEPTION OF NAVGEM, WHICH IS THE SOLE OUTLIER TRACKING THE 
SYSTEM OVER SAMOA NEAR TAU 60. THE JTWC FORECAST TRACK HAS SHIFTED AWAY 
FROM AMERICAN SAMOA DUE TO THE DECOUPLED LLCC'S RECENT WESTWARD TRACK 
MOTION. THE JTWC TRACK FORECAST IS POSITIONED BETWEEN THE JTWC 
CONSENSUS AND THE AICN TRACKER. AS MENTIONED EARLIER, THERE IS SOME 
UNCERTAINTY IN THE JTWC INTENSITY FORECAST WITH A LARGE SPREAD IN 
RELIABLE INTENSITY GUIDANCE THROUGH TAU 48. IN PARTICULAR, THE HAFS-A 
AND COAMPS-TC (GFS AND NAVGEM INITIAL CONDITIONS) INDICATE A BRIEF 
PERIOD OF SLIGHT INTENSIFICATION TO ABOUT 50 KNOTS FROM TAU 0 TO TAU 
12, WHICH SEEMS UNLIKELY DUE TO THE EXTENSIVE DRY AIR PRESENT AND 
PERSISTENT HIGH VWS.
