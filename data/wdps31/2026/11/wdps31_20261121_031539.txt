3. FORECAST REASONING.

SIGNIFICANT FORECAST CHANGES: THERE ARE NO SIGNIFICANT CHANGES TO
THE FORECAST FROM THE PREVIOUS WARNING.

FORECAST DISCUSSION: TC 04P IS EXPECTED TO GRADUALLY WEAKEN UNDER
THE INFLUENCE OF PERSISTENT, MODERATE TO STRONG EASTERLY VERTICAL
WIND SHEAR AND DRY AIR ENTRAINMENT THROUGHOUT THE FORECAST PERIOD. 
BURSTS OF DEEP CONVECTION SUPPORTED BY DIVERGENT FLOW ALOFT WILL
LIKELY FORM AND DISSIPATE OVER THE CIRCULATION AS IT SLOWLY SPINS
DOWN OVER THE WARM (27-29C) SEA SURFACE. THE SYSTEM WILL CONTINUE
ON A GENERALLY WEST-SOUTHWESTWARD TRACK THROUGH TAU 60 UNDER THE
INFLUENCE OF LOW- TO MID-LEVEL SUBTROPICAL RIDGING TO THE
SOUTHEAST. THEREAFTER, SUBTROPICAL RIDGING BUILDING TO THE SOUTHWEST 
WILL DEFLECT THE DISSIPATED SYSTEM WESTWARD AND EVENTUALLY CARRY THE 
REMNANT CIRCULATION WEST-NORTHWESTWARD BEYOND THE FORECAST PERIOD. 

MODEL DISCUSSION: NUMERICAL MODEL GUIDANCE REMAINS IN GOOD
AGREEMENT REGARDING THE OVERALL TRACK FORECAST SCENARIO, PRIMARILY
DEPICTING AN INITIALLY WEST-SOUTHWESTWARD TRACK FOLLOWED BY A GRADUAL 
WESTWARD TURN BY THE END OF THE FORECAST PERIOD. HOWEVER, THERE IS 
STILL NOTEWORTHY SPREAD IN ORIENTATION AND SPEED OF THE SOLUTIONS TO 
DIFFERING DEPICTIONS OF THE STEERING PATTERN EVOLUTION AND 
DEPENDENCIES ON STORM INTENSITY. THE FORECAST ENVELOPE IS SIMILAR TO 
THE PREVIOUS CYCLE AND ALL FORECASTED CENTER POSITIONS REMAIN TO THE 
NORTH OF AMERICAN SAMOA. HOWEVER, ASSOCIATED GALE-FORCE WINDS ARE AND 
WILL REMAIN MOST EXTENSIVE ON THE SOUTHERN SIDE OF THE SYSTEM AND 
COULD AFFECT THE TERRITORY BY TAU 60. THE OPERATIONAL FNV3 AND 
EXPERIMENTAL WNV3 GOOGLE DEEPMIND MACHINE LEARNING WEATHER PREDICTION 
MODEL SOLUTIONS DEPICT DISSIPATION BELOW THE WARNING THRESHOLD OF 35 
KTS WITHIN 24 HOURS, WHILE SKILLFUL PHYSICS-BASED MODELS, INCLUDING 
THE 
HAFS AND COAMPS-TC PREDICT MORE GRADUAL WEAKENING. THE CURRENT 
FORECAST ANTICIPATES A SLOW SPIN DOWN IN LINE WITH THE PHYSICS-BASED 
MODELS AND THE PREVIOUS FORECAST, BUT IT IS WORTH NOTING THAT AN 
EARLIER DISSIPATION IS POSSIBLE.
