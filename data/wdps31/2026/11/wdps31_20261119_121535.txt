SATELLITE ANALYSIS, INITIAL POSITION AND INTENSITY DISCUSSION:
ANIMATED ENHANCED INFRARED (EIR) SATELLITE IMAGERY DEPICTS TROPICAL
CYCLONE (TC) 04P (XAVIER) WITH AN INCREASINGLY CLOUD-FREE EYE OVER
THE LAST SIX HOURS, ALTHOUGH IN THE LAST HOUR IT HAS BEGUN TO
SOMEWHAT FILL ONCE MORE. A REVIEW OF MICROWAVE DATA OVER THE PAST 18
HOURS CONFIRMS XAVIER LIKELY COMPLETED AN EYEWALL REPLACEMENT CYCLE
(ERC), AS A 190524Z WSFM SUITE RESOLVES A SINGLE, COMPACT, ROBUST
EYEWALL WITH AN EYE MEASURING 15-20 NM IN DIAMETER, IN COMPARISON TO 
AN EARLIER 190017Z AMSR-3 PASS WHICH DEPICTED THE PARTIALLY ERODED 
INNER EYEWALL SURROUNDED BY AN INCOMPLETE SECONDARY EYEWALL, MEASURING
ROUGHLY 30 NM IN DIAMETER. THIS SUPPORTS THE BRIEF PERIOD OF
RE-INTENSIFICATION OF 04P OVER THE LAST SIX TO TWELVE HOURS AS THE
SECONDARY EYEWALL BECAME THE PRIMARY EYEWALL AND CONSTRICTED IN,
ALONG WITH THE CLEARING OF THE EYE IN EIR. THE MOST RECENT WSFM
IMAGERY ALSO REVEALS THAT THE UPPER-LEVEL EYE FEATURE REMAINS
SLIGHTLY DISPLACED TO THE NORTHEAST WITH HEIGHT FROM THE LOW-LEVEL
CIRCULATION CENTER (LLCC), INDICATING MORE SOUTHWESTERLY SHEAR THAN 
INDICATED BY MODEL AND CIMSS ANALYSIS. ENVIRONMENTAL CONDITIONS ARE 
ASSESSED AS MARGINALLY FAVORABLE WITH LOW TO MODERATE (10-15 KTS) 
VERTICAL WIND SHEAR (VWS), STRONG POLEWARD OUTFLOW ALOFT, AND LOWERING 
OCEAN HEAT CONTENT (OHC) AS TC 04P HAS TRANSITED INTO AN AREA OF 100 
TO 75 KJ PER CM SQUARED. THE INITIAL POSITION IS ASSESSED WITH HIGH
CONFIDENCE BASED ON THE TIMELY MICROWAVE DATA AND EYE FEATURE. THE
INITIAL INTENSITY IS ASSESSED WITH MEDIUM CONFIDENCE, IN BETWEEN
AGENCY DVORAK FIXES AND ON THE HIGHER END OF CIMSS OBJECTIVE
ESTIMATES AS THE ERC CAUSES BRIEF, TEMPORARY INTENSIFICATION.

3. FORECAST REASONING.

SIGNIFICANT FORECAST CHANGES: THERE ARE NO SIGNIFICANT CHANGES TO
THE FORECAST FROM THE PREVIOUS WARNING.

FORECAST DISCUSSION: TC 04P HAS INCREASED SLIGHTLY IN TRACK SPEED
AS IT CONTINUES SOUTHEASTWARD WITHIN THE WEAK STEERING ENVIRONMENT
OF A COL BETWEEN THE TWO DEEP-LAYER SUBTROPICAL RIDGES (STRS)
CENTERED TO THE EAST AND WEST. A SLOW, MEANDERING TRACK WITHIN THIS
COL IS FORECAST FOR THE NEXT 24 HOURS OR SO BEFORE AN ADDITIONAL
WEAK STR CENTERED TO THE SOUTHEAST IS EXPECTED TO BUILD, CAUSING AN
ACCELERATION SOUTHWESTWARD. THE FORECAST TRACK IS TIGHTLY COUPLED
TO THE FORECAST INTENSITY, AS THE STRENGTH OF XAVIER WILL DETERMINE 
ITS DEPTH AND THUS WHAT LEVEL OF THE STEERING FLOW WILL DOMINATE. 
AFTER TAU 24, AN INCOMING MID-LATITUDE TROUGH IS EXPECTED TO WEAKEN 
THE SOUTHEASTERN STR, WHICH IN COMBINATION WITH THE FORECAST WEAKENING 
OF THE SYSTEM AT THIS POINT WILL CAUSE XAVIER TO BECOME PRIMARILY 
STEERED BY THE LOW TO MID LEVEL STR CENTERED TO THE SOUTHEAST. BETWEEN 
TAUS 96 AND 120, THE TRACK IS EXPECTED TO BECOME MORE DIRECTLY 
WESTWARD AS AN STR CENTERED TO THE SOUTHWEST TAKES OVER STEERING 
INFLUENCE AS THE MID-LATITUDE TROUGH WEAKENS THE SOUTHEASTERN STR. 
REGARDING INTENSITY, TC 04P IS EXPECTED TO WEAKEN FOR THE REMAINDER OF 
THE FORECAST PERIOD AS IT CONTINUES TRANSITING INTO LOWER OHCS AND ITS 
CURRENT SLOW TRACK SPEED INDUCES UPWELLING OF COLDER SSTS. BEYOND TAU 
24, THE WEAKENING TREND WILL BECOME MUCH SHARPER AS VWS BECOMES 
INCREASINGLY HOSTILE, EVENTUALLY DECOUPLING THE UPPER AND LOWER 
VORTEXES AND ALLOWING DRY AIR ENTRAINMENT INTO THE SHALLOWER CORE. THE 
CIRCULATION IS EXPECTED TO OPEN UP AND BEGIN DISSIPATING BY TAU 120, 
ALTHOUGH SOME GLOBAL MODEL FIELDS SUGGEST THIS COULD HAPPEN EARLIER.

MODEL DISCUSSION: TRACK MODEL GUIDANCE IS IN GOOD AGREEMENT OUT TO
TAU 72 (WITH THE EXCEPTIONS OF NAVGEM AND UKMET ENSEMBLE), WITH 70
NM CROSS-TRACK SPREAD AND A SLIGHTLY LARGER ALONG-TRACK SPREAD OF
115 NM. BEYOND THIS POINT, MODELS DIVERGE IN THE SHARPNESS OF THE
WESTWARD TURN BETWEEN TAU 72 AND 96, WITH CROSS-TRACK SPREAD
EXPANDING TO 235 NM AT TAU 120, WITH THE BULK OF MEMBERS ROUGHLY
CENTERED OVER AMERICAN SAMOA. ALONG-TRACK SPREAD ALSO GREW TO 185
NM, WITH EXPERIMENTAL GOOGLE DEEPMIND WNV-3 BEING THE SLOWEST AND 
STILL FAR NORTHEAST OF AMERICAN SAMOA, WHILE THE FASTEST OF GALWEM AND 
GEFS REACH THE ISLAND OF UPOLU BY TAU 120. THE OFFICIAL JTWC FORECAST 
TRACK IS ASSESSED WITH MEDIUM CONFIDENCE OUT TO TAU 72 AND LOW 
CONFIDENCE THEREAFTER, CLOSELY IN LINE WITH THE MULTI-MODEL AND AI 
CONSENSUS THROUGHOUT. INTENSITY MODEL GUIDANCE IS MORE SPREAD, WITH 
GOOGLE DEEPMIND, DECAY SHIPS, AND HAFS-A FORECASTING A BRIEF PLATEAU 
TO SMALL WEAKENING OUT TO TAU 24 FOLLOWED BY SHARPER WEAKENING 
THEREAFTER (EXCLUDING DECAY SHIPS, WHICH SHOWS A MORE CONSTANT 
WEAKENING THROUGHOUT, BECOMING THE UPPER-LEVEL OUTLIER). GFS AND HWRF 
WEAKEN THE SYSTEM MORE IMMINENTLY, LOWERING 04P BELOW TYPHOON 
INTENSITY BY TAU 24. ALL MODELS AGREE ON A LESS SHARP WEAKENING AFTER 
TAU 48, CONVERGING BETWEEN 25-40 KTS BY TAU 120. THE OFFICIAL JTWC 
FORECAST INTENSITY IS ASSESSED WITH MEDIUM CONFIDENCE THROUGHOUT, 
CLOSELY IN LINE WITH BOTH GOOGLE DEEPMINDS WHICH ALIGN WITH THE JTWC 
FORECAST TRACK OUT TO TAU 96 AND THEN FALLING BELOW THEM AFTERWARDS AS 
THE JTWC TRACK OUTPACES THAT OF GOOGLE DEEPMIND.
