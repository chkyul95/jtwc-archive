3. FORECAST REASONING.

SIGNIFICANT FORECAST CHANGES: THERE ARE NO SIGNIFICANT CHANGES TO
THE FORECAST FROM THE PREVIOUS WARNING.

FORECAST DISCUSSION: TC 04P APPEARS TO BE STARTING ON THE
ANTICIPATED WEST-SOUTHWESTWARD TRACK ALONG THE NORTHWESTERN
PERIPHERY OF THE STR. THE WEST-SOUTHWESTWARD TRAJECTORY IS FORECAST
TO PERSIST THROUGH TAU 24. NEAR TAU 24, THE STR WILL PROPAGATE EAST
DUE TO AN INCOMING SHORTWAVE TROUGH AND A SEPARATE STR WILL BUILD 
NORTH OF NEW ZEALAND, REDIRECTING 04P NORTHWESTWARD THROUGH THE 
REMAINDER OF THE FORECAST PERIOD (TAU 48). REGARDING INTENSITY, 04P 
IS FORECAST TO GRADUALLY WEAKEN THROUGHOUT THE FORECAST PERIOD IN 
RESPONSE TO PERSISTENT VERTICAL WIND SHEAR AND DRY AIR ENTRAINMENT. 
HOWEVER, THE PERIODIC DEVELOPMENT OF CONVECTION COULD ALLOW FOR A 
MORE SINUSOIDAL INTENSITY TREND OVER THE NEXT 12-24 HOURS. FOLLOWING 
TAU 24 DRY AIR INTRUSION WILL WORSEN, CAUSING THE VORTEX OF 04P TO 
BECOME COMPLETELY DECOUPLED AND SHALLOW. THE SYSTEM IS THEN FORECAST 
TO DISSIPATE OVER WATER NEAR TAU 48, NORTH OF SAMOA. 

MODEL DISCUSSION: TRACK GUIDANCE IS IN SLIGHTLY WORSE AGREEMENT
THIS MODEL RUN, MOSTLY DUE TO THE PRESENCE OF TWO OUTLIERS. WHILE
NAVGEM CONTINUES TO BE A SIGNIFICANT POLEWARD OUTLIER, THE ECMWF
DETERMINISTIC IS NOW ALSO AN OUTLIER. THE ECMWF SHOWS 04P RAPIDLY
WEAKENING AND STALLING NORTH OF AMERICAN SAMOA AROUND TAU 48 RATHER
THAN CONTINUING ON THE NORTHWESTWARD TURN. WITH THE EXCEPTION OF
THESE TWO MODELS, TRACK GUIDANCE REMAINS CONSISTENT WITH THE
PREVIOUS GUIDANCE PACKAGE. THE CROSS-TRACK SPREAD AT TAU 48 IS
MEASURED TO BE ABOUT 70 NM AT TAU 48 WHEN DISCOUNTING NAVGEM. THE
JTWC TRACK FORECAST IS PLACED JUST NORTH OF THE CONSENSUS, SIMILAR
TO THE PREVIOUS FORECAST, WITH MEDIUM CONFIDENCE. INTENSITY
GUIDANCE GENERALLY AGREES ON WEAKENING FROM TAU 12 ONWARD. IT IS
WORTH NOTING THAT BOTH GFS AND ECMWF WEAKEN THE SYSTEM FASTER THAN
THE MESOSCALE MODELS DO, SHOWING AN INTENSITY BELOW TC-STRENGTH BY
TAU 24. THE JTWC INTENSITY FORECAST IS SET NEAR COAMPS-TC AND HWRF
THROUGHOUT THE SHORT FORECAST PERIOD WITH MEDIUM CONFIDENCE.
