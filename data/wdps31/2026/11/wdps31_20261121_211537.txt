SATELLITE ANALYSIS, INITIAL POSITION AND INTENSITY DISCUSSION:
ANIMATED MULTISPECTRAL SATELLITE IMAGERY (MSI) DEPICTS AN EXPOSED,
WELL-DEFINED LOW-LEVEL CIRCULATION (LLC) WITH FLARING DEEP
CONVECTION OVER THE EASTERN QUADRANT OF THE LLC. CONSEQUENTLY, THE
INITIAL POSITION IS PLACED WITH HIGH CONFIDENCE BASED ON THE MSI.
AS REVEALED IN THE 211617Z SSMIS 91GHZ MICROWAVE IMAGE, ALL THE
DEEP CONVECTION IS SHEARED OVER THE EASTERN PERIPHERY OF THE SYSTEM
DUE TO PERSISTENT WEST-SOUTHWESTERLY TO SOUTHWESTERLY HIGH (25-30
KNOTS) VERTICAL WIND SHEAR, WITH SHALLOW BANDING WRAPPING INTO THE
LOW-LEVEL CIRCULATION CENTER. A 211633Z RCM-2 SAR IMAGE INDICATES
THAT THE STRONGEST BAND OF GALE FORCE WINDS (40-45 KNOTS) ARE
CONFINED TO THE SOUTHEAST QUADRANT OUT TO 30-35NM. THE BORDERLINE
GALE-FORCE WINDS (30-35 KNOTS) EXTEND FARTHER OUT TO THE EAST AND
SOUTH. THE INITIAL INTENSITY IS ASSESSED AT 45 KNOTS BASED ON THE
RCM-2 SAR DATA, WHICH SHOWS A VMAX OF 42-45 KNOTS. DVORAK ESTIMATES
RANGE FROM T3.0-3.5 LARGELY DUE TO THE SHORT-LIVED CENTRAL DENSE
OVERCAST THAT HAD FORMED OVER THE CENTER PRIOR TO THE RECENT RAPID
WEAKENING PHASE.

3. FORECAST REASONING.

SIGNIFICANT FORECAST CHANGES: FORECAST SHIFTED SLIGHTLY (10NM)
NORTH AT TAU 48 AND TAU 60

FORECAST DISCUSSION: TROPICAL CYCLONE (TC) 04P IS FORECAST TO
MAINTAIN A WEST-SOUTHWESTWARD TRACK MOTION THROUGH TAU 36 AS IT
TRACKS ALONG THE NORTHWEST PERIPHERY OF THE LOW- TO MID-LEVEL
SUBTROPICAL RIDGE. THE SYSTEM SHOULD MAINTAIN AN INTENSITY RANGING
FROM 40-45 KNOTS THROUGH TAU 36 ALTHOUGH A FASTER WEAKENING TREND
IS POSSIBLE DUE TO THE UNFAVORABLE ENVIRONMENT CHARACTERIZED BY
EXTENSIVE DRY AIR AND MODERATE TO HIGH VERTICAL WIND SHEAR. AFTER
TAU 36, THE SYSTEM IS EXPECTED TO TURN SHARPLY WEST-NORTHWESTWARD
ALONG THE NORTHERN PERIPHERY OF A STRONG HIGH FORECAST TO BUILD TO
THE SOUTH. TC 04P WILL LIKELY MAINTAIN EXTENSIVE GALE-FORCE WINDS
OVER THE SOUTHERN SEMICIRCLE DUE TO THE STRONG PRESSURE GRADIENT
ASSOCIATED WITH THE HIGH, WHICH WILL CONTINUE TO IMPACT AMERICAN
SAMOA THROUGH THE FORECAST PERIOD.

MODEL DISCUSSION: IN GENERAL, THE AI-BASED MODELS INDICATE A MORE
RAPID WEAKENING TREND AND AN EARLIER, SHARPER WEST-NORTHWESTWARD
TURN. THE PHYSICS-BASED MODELS ARE CLUSTERED ON A MORE PROLONGED
WEST-SOUTHWESTWARD TRACK AND WESTWARD TURN NEAR TAU 48 WELL NORTH
OF AMERICAN SAMOA. THE JTWC FORECAST HAS MAINTAINED A CONSISTENT
TRACK AND INTENSITY FORECAST, WHICH IS POSITIONED BETWEEN THE JTWC
CONSENSUS AND THE AICN TRACKER. AS MENTIONED EARLIER, THERE IS SOME
UNCERTAINTY IN THE JTWC INTENSITY FORECAST WITH A LARGE SPREAD IN
RELIABLE INTENSITY GUIDANCE THROUGH TAU 48. IN PARTICULAR, THE
HAFS-A AND COAMPS-TC (GFS AND NAVGEM INITIAL CONDITIONS) INDICATE A
BRIEF PERIOD OF INTENSIFICATION TO 50-55 KNOTS FROM TAU 12 TO TAU
36, WHICH SEEMS UNLIKELY DUE TO THE EXTENSIVE DRY AIR PRESENT
THROUGH THE FORECAST PERIOD.
