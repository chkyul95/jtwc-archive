3. FORECAST REASONING.

SIGNIFICANT FORECAST CHANGES: THERE ARE NO SIGNIFICANT CHANGES TO
THE FORECAST FROM THE PREVIOUS WARNING.

FORECAST DISCUSSION: TC 04P IS FORECAST TO IMMINENTLY BEGIN A
POLEWARD TURN, TRACKING ALONG THE WESTERN PERIPHERY OF THE STR TO
THE EAST. A SLOW POLEWARD TRACK IS THEN FORECAST TO PERSIST THROUGH
TAU 48. AFTERWARD, THE STR WILL RETREAT EASTWARD, LEAVING 04P
PLACED WITHIN A WEAK STEERING PATTERN. AS A RESULT, THE SYSTEM IS
FORECAST TO REMAIN QUASI-STATIONARY FOR ABOUT 24 HOURS. NEAR TAU
72, THE VORTEX IS EXPECTED TO BECOME SHALLOW, CAUSING THE STEERING
TO BE DOMINATED BY A LOW TO MID-LEVEL STR SITUATED TO THE
SOUTHEAST, ALLOWING FOR TRACK SPEEDS TO INCREASE IN THE
WEST-SOUTHWESTWARD DIRECTION THROUGH THE REMAINDER OF THE FORECAST
PERIOD. IN TERMS OF INTENSITY, 04P IS EXPECTED TO CURRENTLY BE AT
OR NEAR ITS PEAK INTENSITY. WESTERLY SHEAR IS ANTICIPATED TO BEGIN
GRADUALLY INCREASING ALONG WITH THE UPWELLING OF COLD WATER DUE TO
THE SLOW TRACK SPEEDS. THESE TWO FACTORS WILL CAUSE 04P TO WEAKEN
AS IT TRACKS SLOWLY POLEWARD. AROUND TAU 60, DRY AIR ENTRAINMENT
FROM THE WEST AND NORTH WILL INCREASE, FURTHER AIDING IN WEAKENING
THE SYSTEM THROUGH THE REMAINDER OF THE FORECAST PERIOD. 

MODEL DISCUSSION: MODEL TRACK GUIDANCE IS IN RELATIVELY GOOD
AGREEMENT THROUGH TUA 72 WITH THE VAST MAJORITY OF MODELS AGREEING
ON THE SOUTH POLEWARD TRACK. AFTER TAU 72, MODELS BEGIN TO GREATLY
DIVERGE. THERE ARE TWO EXTREME OUTLIERS IN GALWEM AND NAVGEM, WHICH
ARE SIGNIFICANTLY FURTHER EQUATORWARD AND POLEWARD RESPECTIVELY.
DISCOUNTING THE TWO OUTLIERS, THERE IS A 180 NM CROSS-TRACK SPREAD
AND 260 NM ALONG-TRACK SPREAD AT TAU 120. THE FASTEST MEMBER IS
MADE UP OF THE GFS ENSEMBLE MEAN WHILE THE SLOWEST IS THE ECMWF
ENSEMBLE MEAN. THE JTWC TRACK FORECAST IS PLACED NEAR THE
MULTI-MODEL CONSENSUS THROUGH TAU 72 AND THEN CLOSER TO THE GOOGLE
DEEPMIND AND ECMWF DETERMINISTIC TRACKERS THROUGH TAU 120.
INTENSITY GUIDANCE GENERALLY AGREES ON A STEADY OR SLIGHT WEAKENING
THROUGH TAU 12 FOLLOWED BY WEAKENING. THE JTWC INTENSITY FORECAST
IS PLACED NEAR THE CONSENSUS THROUGHOUT THE FORECAST PERIOD WITH
OVERALL MEDIUM CONFIDENCE.
