SATELLITE ANALYSIS, INITIAL POSITION AND INTENSITY DISCUSSION:
ANIMATED ENHANCED INFRARED (EIR) SATELLITE IMAGERY DEPICTS A COMPACT 
TROPICAL CYCLONE (TC) WITH A RAPIDLY CLOUD-FILLING EYE AND WARMING 
CONVECTIVE CLOUD TOPS. THE EYE FEATURE BECAME INCREASINGLY RAGGED AS 
EARLY AS 1200Z AND SUBSEQUENTLY BECAME COMPLETELY OBSCURED BY 1800Z. 
HOWEVER, THE INNER-CORE EYE REMAINS WELL DEFINED IN A SERIES OF 
MICROWAVE PASSES, INCLUDING A 191629Z WSF-M 37 GHZ IMAGE, THOUGH THE 
LOWER-LEVEL MICROWAVE EYE IS LESS DISTINCT IN THE 191742Z SSMIS 37 
GHZ IMAGE DUE TO THE COARSER SPATIAL RESOLUTION OF THE SENSOR. A 
191632Z SENTINEL-1C SYNTHETIC APERTURE RADAR (SAR) IMAGE REVEALS A 
COMPACT, SYMMETRIC SURFACE WIND FIELD SURROUNDING A WELL-DEFINED LOW-
LEVEL CIRCULATION CENTER (LLCC), WITH A NOAA STAR-ANALYZED MEAN VMAX 
OF 94 KNOTS AND A PEAK VMAX OF 103 KNOTS. THE INITIAL POSITION IS 
ASSESSED WITH HIGH CONFIDENCE BASED ON AN EXTRAPOLATION OF THE 
MICROWAVE- AND SAR-DERIVED LLCC POSITIONS. THE INITIAL INTENSITY IS 
ASSESSED WITH HIGH CONFIDENCE BASED ON A BLEND OF THE SUBJECTIVE AND 
OBJECTIVE INTENSITY ESTIMATES OUTLINED BELOW, CORROBORATED BY THE SAR 
DATA. ENVIRONMENTAL CONDITIONS ARE MARGINAL, CHARACTERIZED BY 
FAVORABLE SEA SURFACE TEMPERATURES (SSTS), MODERATE POLEWARD OUTFLOW 
(CONFINED TO THE SOUTHEASTERN QUADRANT), AND VERTICAL WIND SHEAR 
(VWS) THAT IS CURRENTLY ANALYZED AS LOW TO MODERATE, BUT IS RAPIDLY 
INCREASING, PARTICULARLY IN THE MID-LEVELS.

INITIAL WIND RADII BASIS: SCATTEROMETER AND SAR DATA.

3. FORECAST REASONING.

SIGNIFICANT FORECAST CHANGES: THERE ARE NO SIGNIFICANT CHANGES TO
THE FORECAST FROM THE PREVIOUS WARNING.

FORECAST DISCUSSION: TROPICAL CYCLONE (TC) 04P CONTINUES TO DRIFT 
SLOWLY EAST-SOUTHEASTWARD AS IT REMAINS QUASI-STATIONARY WITHIN A 
WEAK STEERING PATTERN. THE SYSTEM LIES WITHIN AN UPPER-TROPOSPHERIC 
COL, WHICH IS BEING DISPLACED EASTWARD BY THE APPROACH OF A DEEP 
UPPER-LEVEL TROUGH FROM THE WEST. THE FORECAST TRACK FOR TC 04P IS 
TIGHTLY COUPLED WITH THE INTENSITY FORECAST AND WILL BE GOVERNED BY 
CHANGES IN VERTICAL VORTEX DEPTH. AS MENTIONED ABOVE, MID-LEVEL SHEAR 
IS ALREADY INCREASING SHARPLY, ESTIMATED AT 25 KNOTS AT 1800Z. GLOBAL 
MODEL-DERIVED SOUNDINGS AND SHIPS STATISTICAL GUIDANCE INDICATE THAT 
DEEP-LAYER SHEAR MAGNITUDES WILL DOUBLE WITHIN THE NEXT 12 TO 24 
HOURS, ACCOMPANIED BY SEVERE MID-TROPOSPHERIC DRY AIR INTRUSION. AS A 
RESULT OF THE INTENSIFYING SHEAR AND DRY AIR ENTRAINMENT, THE VORTEX 
WILL UNDERGO VERTICAL DECOUPLING WITHIN THE NEXT 24 HOURS. AS THE 
VORTEX REDUCES IN VERTICAL EXTENT, IT WILL TRANSITION TO THE STEERING 
INFLUENCE OF PROGRESSIVELY LOWER LEVELS OF THE TROPOSPHERE AND 
DEFLECT SHARPLY SOUTHWESTWARD ALONG THE NORTHWESTERN PERIPHERY OF A 
LOW- TO MID-LEVEL STR CENTERED TO THE SOUTHEAST. THE SOUTHWESTWARD 
TRAJECTORY IS FORECAST TO CONTINUE THROUGH THE REMAINDER OF THE 
FORECAST PERIOD, BRINGING THE CENTER OF TC 04P IN CLOSE PROXIMITY TO 
THE MANUA ISLANDS AND JUST EAST OF THE MAIN ISLANDS OF AMERICAN SAMOA 
WITHIN 96 HOURS. THE SYSTEM WILL GRADUALLY WEAKEN OVER THE NEXT 12 
HOURS AS IT EXPERIENCES THE COMBINED EFFECTS OF INCREASED MID-LEVEL 
SHEAR AND LOCALIZED OCEANIC UPWELLING. FOLLOWING VERTICAL VORTEX 
DECOUPLING, THE SYSTEM WILL RAPIDLY WEAKEN THROUGH TAU 60, BEFORE 
WEAKENING AT A MORE MODEST RATE THROUGH THE REMAINDER OF THE FORECAST 
PERIOD.  

MODEL DISCUSSION: DETERMINISTIC AND ENSEMBLE TRACK GUIDANCE IS IN 
RELATIVELY GOOD AGREEMENT REGARDING THE OVERALL TRACK SCENARIO, WITH 
ALL MEMBERS OF THE TRACK GUIDANCE PACKAGE DEPICTING A TURN SOUTHWARD 
THEN SOUTHWESTWARD OVER THE NEXT 24 HOURS. HOWEVER, SIGNIFICANT 
BIFURCATION BECOMES APPARENT AFTER TAU 72 AS THE MODELS DIVERGE ON 
THE ORIENTATION OF THE LOW-LEVEL RIDGE TO THE SOUTH. AT TAU 72, 
CROSS-TRACK SPREAD IS ROUGHLY 120 NM, BOUNDED BY THE GEFS MEAN ON THE 
NORTHERN EDGE OF THE GUIDANCE ENVELOPE AND THE GALWEM, GOOGLE 
DEEPMIND (GDMI), AND ECEPS MEAN ON THE SOUTHERN EDGE. CROSS-TRACK 
SPREAD INCREASES TO 240 NM BY TAU 120, BOUNDED BY THE EXPERIMENTAL 
WINDBORNE AI MODEL ON THE NORTHERN EDGE OF THE ENVELOPE (PLACING THE 
CENTER NEAR SWAINS ISLAND) AND THE ECMWF, GALWEM, AND GDMI ON THE 
SOUTHERN EDGE (TRACKING THE CENTER CLOSER TO AMERICAN SAMOA). 
ENSEMBLE GUIDANCE REMAINS HIGHLY DISPERSED, WITH SIGNIFICANT INTRA-
ENSEMBLE MEMBER DISPERSION SPANNING BETWEEN SWAINS ISLAND, WALLIS AND 
FUTUNA, AND THE COOK ISLANDS. THE OFFICIAL JTWC FORECAST IS 
POSITIONED NEAR THE ECMWF AND AI CONSENSUS (AICN) THROUGH THE 
FORECAST PERIOD, WITH MEDIUM CONFIDENCE IN THE NEAR-TERM AND LOW 
CONFIDENCE IN THE EXTENDED-RANGE FORECAST. INTENSITY GUIDANCE IS IN 
GOOD AGREEMENT, DEPICTING A GENERAL WEAKENING TREND THROUGH THE 
ENTIRE FORECAST PERIOD, THOUGH NOTABLE DIFFERENCES EXIST REGARDING 
THE RATE OF WEAKENING. HAFS-A, HWRF, AND THE GDMI ARE AMONG THE 
MODELS DEPICTING AN EXTREMELY RAPID WEAKENING TREND THROUGH TAU 60 
FOLLOWED BY A MORE GRADUAL DECAY THROUGH TAU 120, WHILE BOTH DECAY-
SHIPS SOLUTIONS (NAVGEM AND GFS) DEPICT A MORE CONSERVATIVE WEAKENING 
PACE THROUGH THE FORECAST PERIOD. THE JTWC INTENSITY FORECAST IS 
PLACED CLOSE TO THE GDMI SOLUTION WITH MEDIUM CONFIDENCE.
