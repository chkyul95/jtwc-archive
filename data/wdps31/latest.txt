FORECAST DISCUSSION: TC 04P IS FORECAST TO CONTINUE SOUTHWESTWARD
OUT TO TAU 60, STEERED INITIALLY BY A DEEP-LAYER SUBTROPICAL RIDGE
(STR) CENTERED TO THE SOUTHEAST, WITH STEERING INFLUENCE
TRANSITIONING TO A MID TO LOW LEVEL STR CENTERED TO THE SOUTHEAST
AS THE INTENSITY OF XAVIER CONTINUES TO DECREASE. BY TAU 60, AN
INCOMING MID-LATITUDE TROUGH IS EXPECTED TO WEAKEN THE SOUTHEASTERN
STR AND SHIFT IT EASTWARD, ALLOWING THE LOW-LEVEL SOUTHEASTERLY
TRADE WIND FLOW TO DOMINATE STEERING THEREAFTER, CAUSING AN
EQUATORWARD TURN BETWEEN TAUS 60 AND 72. THE TRACK AND INTENSITY
FORECASTS REMAIN CLOSELY COUPLED, WITH THE POSSIBILITY THAT A FASTER 
WEAKENING SYSTEM THAN FORECAST WILL TURN EQUATORWARD SOONER AS THE 
LOW-LEVEL FLOW TAKES OVER THE STEERING OF THE VORTEX. REGARDING 
INTENSITY, A RELATIVELY STEADY WEAKENING TREND IS EXPECTED FOR THE 
REMAINDER OF THE FORECAST PERIOD, WITH AN INITIALLY SHARPER DECREASE 
FORECAST OUT TO TAU 12 AS THE VORTEX CONTINUES TO DECOUPLE AND 
TRANSITS INTO AN AREA OF LOWER OHC. VWS IS ALSO EXPECTED TO REMAIN 
ABOVE 30 KTS BY TAU 24, CAUSING FURTHER DRY AIR ENTRAINMENT INTO THE 
SHALLOW CIRCULATION. THE CIRCULATION IS EXPECTED TO BEGIN DISSIPATING 
AT TAU 72 AND DISSIPATE NO LATER THAN TAU 96 AS IT PASSES NORTH OF 
AMERICAN SAMOA, WITH AN ASYMMETRICAL WIND FIELD COMPRISED OF HIGHEST 
WINDS ON THE SOUTHERN FLANK.

MODEL DISCUSSION: MODEL TRACK GUIDANCE IS IN FAIR AGREEMENT BUT HAS
SPREAD SLIGHTLY SINCE THE PREVIOUS CYCLE, WITH THE ASSOCIATED 
INTENSITY FORECASTS OF EACH MODEL GREATLY IMPACTING THE TIMING AND 
SEVERITY OF THE STEERING FLOW ON THE SYSTEM. THE EXPERIMENTAL GOOGLE
DEEPMIND COMPRISES THE MOST EQUATORWARD AND SLOWEST SOLUTION WHILE
GALWEM, GEFS, AIFS, AND UKMET ENSEMBLE COMPRISE THE FASTEST
SOLUTIONS, BRINGING THE SYSTEM WELL NORTHWEST OF SAMOA BY TAU 96.
CROSS-TRACK SPREAD AT THE TIME OF CPA TO PAGO PAGO IS NEARLY 200
NM, ALTHOUGH THE TIGHTEST GROUPING OF MODELS AT THAT POINT
(REMOVING OUTLIERS OF WNV-3 AND NAVGEM) BRINGS THIS TO 75 NM, WITH
NAVGEM AND GALWEM BEING THE ONLY MODELS TO SHOW A TRACK SOUTH OF
AMERICAN SAMOA. ALONG-TRACK SPREAD AT TAU 72 IS 50 NM AND INCREASES
THEREAFTER, LOWERING CONFIDENCE IN REGARDS TO THE TIMING TO CPA OF 
AMERICAN SAMOA. THE OFFICIAL JTWC FORECAST TRACK IS THEREFORE LAID 
WITH MEDIUM CONFIDENCE OUT TO TAU 72 AND LOW CONFIDENCE THEREAFTER,
HEDGED BETWEEN THE ADJUSTED MULTI-MODEL (EXCLUDING NAVGEM) AND AI
CONSENSUS THROUGHOUT. MODEL INTENSITY GUIDANCE IS IN GOOD AGREEMENT
ON WEAKENING FOR THE REMAINDER OF THE FORECAST PERIOD, WITH GFS,
HWRF, AND BOTH GOOGLE DEEPMINDS BEING THE MOST AGGRESSIVE IN THE
SHORT TERM FOLLOWED BY A PLATEAU, WHILE DECAY SHIPS IS A STEADIER
TREND OUT TO TAU 96. THE OFFICIAL JTWC INTENSITY FORECAST IS
ASSESSED WITH MEDIUM CONFIDENCE THROUGHOUT, CLOSELY IN LINE WITH
THE DECAY SHIPS SOLUTION THROUGHOUT AS THE ASSOCIATED TRACK ALIGNS
WELL WITH THE JTWC FORECAST.
