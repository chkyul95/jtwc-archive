ANIMATED ENHANCED INFRARED (EIR) SATELLITE IMAGERY DEPICTS TROPICAL
CYCLONE (TC) 04P (XAVIER) WITH AN INCREASINGLY ASYMMETRICAL CENTRAL
DENSE OVERCAST (CDO) COMPRISED OF SLIGHTLY WARMING CLOUD TOPS.
WEST-SOUTHWESTERLY VERTICAL WIND SHEAR (VWS) HAS INCREASED TO 25-30
KTS, EVIDENT IN EIR AS OUTFLOW ALOFT IS SUPPRESSED ON THE WESTERN
FLANK AND THE OUTER BANDS OF THE LOW LEVEL CIRCULATION CENTER
(LLCC) BECOME SLIGHTLY EXPOSED. WHILE THE LLCC REMAINS OBSCURED, A
200503Z WSFM 37 GHZ MICROWAVE IMAGE REVEALED THE LLCC TO BE
POSITIONED UNDERNEATH THE NORTHEASTERN QUADRANT OF THE CDO. LATE
RECEIPT OF A 200519Z RCM-3 SAR PASS LENDS CONFIDENCE TO THE INITIAL 
POSITION OF THE SLIGHTLY ELONGATED LLCC AND MORE ASYMMETRICAL WIND
FIELD IN COMPARISON TO PREVIOUS WIND SPEED DATA. THE MICROWAVE AND
SAR DATA ALSO SUPPORT THE STRONGEST CONVECTION AND WIND SPEEDS NOW 
CONFINED TO A BAND EXTENDING TO THE SOUTHEAST OF THE LLCC. THE INITIAL 
POSITION IS PLACED WITH HIGH CONFIDENCE BASED ON THE MICROWAVE AND SAR 
DATA. THE INITIAL INTENSITY IS ASSESSED WITH MEDIUM CONFIDENCE, AS THE 
ADJUSTED WIND SPEED MAXIMA FROM THE SAR PASS INDICATE AN INTENSITY 
CLOSER TO 85 KTS.

3. FORECAST REASONING.

SIGNIFICANT FORECAST CHANGES: THERE ARE NO SIGNIFICANT CHANGES TO
THE FORECAST FROM THE PREVIOUS WARNING.

FORECAST DISCUSSION: TC 04P IS FORECAST TO INCREASE IN TRACK SPEED
AND TRACK SOUTHWESTWARD AS A SUBTROPICAL RIDGE (STR) CENTERED TO
THE SOUTHEAST BECOMES THE PRIMARY STEERING MECHANISM OUT UNTIL TAU
24. AFTER THIS POINT, AN APPROACHING MID-LATITUDE TROUGH IS
EXPECTED TO WEAKEN THE STR. THIS, IN COMBINATION WITH THE FORECAST
DECOUPLING AND WEAKENING OF XAVIER AT THIS POINT WILL CAUSE
STEERING TO BE DOMINATED BY THE MID- TO LOW-LEVEL FLOW,
SPECIFICALLY A STR CENTERED FARTHER TO THE SOUTHEAST, MAINTAINING
THE GENERAL SOUTHWESTWARD TRAJECTORY UNTIL TAU 72. AT THIS POINT,
THE LOW-LEVEL TRADEWIND FLOW WILL SHIFT TO SOUTHEASTERLY FLOW,
CAUSING A NORTHWESTWARD TURN BETWEEN TAUS 72 AND 96. AS A RESULT OF
THE MORE WESTERLY TRACK OVER THE LAST SIX HOURS, THE CPA TO PAGO
PAGO HAS SHIFTED NORTHWARD BY ROUGHLY 30 NM BETWEEN TAUS 72 AND 96,
ALTHOUGH THE TRACK REMAINS UNCERTAIN AT THIS POINT. THE INTENSITY
AND TRACK FORECASTS REMAIN TIGHTLY COUPLED, THUS IF XAVIER WEAKENS 
FASTER THAN CURRENTLY FORECAST, THE NORTHWESTWARD TURN COULD OCCUR 
EARLIER THAN TAU 72. AS THE OFFICIAL JTWC INTENSITY FORECAST STANDS, 
THE SHARPEST WEAKENING TREND WILL OCCUR OUT TO TAU 24 AS VWS CONTINUES 
TO SHARPLY INCREASE, WITH HAFS-A MODEL CROSS-SECTIONS FORECASTING
IMMINENT DE-COUPLING OF THE VORTEX. AFTER THIS POINT, DRY AIR
ENTRAINMENT INTO THE SHALLOWING VORTEX AND PROGRESSION INTO LOWER
(LESS THAN 100 KJ PER CM SQUARED) OCEAN HEAT CONTENT (OHC) WILL
FURTHER WEAKEN THE SYSTEM, WITH DISSIPATION FORECAST TO BEGIN NO
LATER THAN TAU 96 AND COMPLETING BY TAU 120. 

MODEL DISCUSSION: MODEL TRACK GUIDANCE IS IN FAIR AGREEMENT ON AN
OVERALL WEST-SOUTHWESTWARD TRACK OUT TO TAU 60, WITH SOME MODELS
FORECASTING AN EARLIER WESTWARD TURN, TIED TO THEIR QUICKER
WEAKENING OF THE SYSTEM. OVERALL, THE ENTIRE PACKAGE HAS SHIFTED
NORTHWARD WITH THE OUTLIERS OF NAVGEM AND JGSM BEING THE ONLY
MODELS TO BRING XAVIER SOUTH OF AMERICAN SAMOA. GUIDANCE DIVERGES
A BIT AFTER TAU 72, WITH CROSS-TRACK SPREAD INCREASING TO 370 NM
AND ALONG-TRACK SPREAD AT 250 NM BY TAU 120. AI-BASED MODELS
DIVERGE IN TRACK SPEED AT THIS POINT, WITH BOTH GOOGLE DEEPMINDS
LOSING THE VORTEX AT TAU 96 AND AI GEFS AND AI GFS AS THE SLOWEST
MEMBERS OF THE GUIDANCE PACKAGE WHILE AIFS AND EXPERIMENTAL WEATHER
MESH ARE AMONG THE FASTEST. THE OFFICIAL JTWC FORECAST TRACK IS
THUS PLACED WITH MEDIUM CONFIDENCE OUT TO TAU 72 AND LOW CONFIDENCE
THEREAFTER, CLOSELY IN BETWEEN THE MULTI-MODEL AND AI CONSENSUS AND
HEDGING TOWARDS THE FASTER ADJUSTED MULTI-MODEL CONSENSUS
(EXCLUDING NAVGEM) IN THE LATER TAUS. MODEL INTENSITY GUIDANCE IS
IN GOOD AGREEMENT ON WEAKENING FOR THE REMAINDER OF THE FORECAST
PERIOD, WITH GFS AND HWRF BEING THE SHARPEST TO DECLINE WHILE SHIPS
DECAY IS THE MOST MODEST. THE OFFICIAL JTWC INTENSITY FORECAST IS
ASSESSED WITH MEDIUM CONFIDENCE THROUGHOUT, SLIGHTLY BELOW THE
MULTI-MODEL CONSENSUS THROUGHOUT.
