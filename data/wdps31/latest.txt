EXTREMELY COLD, MEASURING COLDER THAN -90 C WITHIN THE OVERSHOOTING

BURST OF CONVECTION DISPLACED ABOUT 40 NM TO THE EAST OF THE
ASSESSED CENTER LOCATION. PERSISTENT WESTERLY SHEAR CONTINUES TO
IMPACT THE SYSTEM AND IS THE PRIMARY CAUSE FOR THE EASTWARD VORTEX
TILT. THE INITIAL POSITION IS PLACED WITH HIGH CONFIDENCE BASED ON
THE AMSR3 AND EIR IMAGERY. THE INITIAL INTENSITY OF 45 KTS IS

3. FORECAST REASONING.

SIGNIFICANT FORECAST CHANGES: THERE ARE NO SIGNIFICANT CHANGES TO
THE FORECAST FROM THE PREVIOUS WARNING.

FORECAST DISCUSSION: TC 04P IS FORECAST TO TRACK
WEST-SOUTHWESTWARD, ALONG THE NORTHWESTERN PERIPHERY OF THE
SUBTROPICAL RIDGE TO THE SOUTHEAST THROUGH TAU 48.  AROUND TAU 48,
A SHORTWAVE TROUGH WILL BREAK DOWN THE CURRENT STEERING RIDGE AND A
SEPARATE RIDGE WILL BUILD IN BEHIND IT, ANCHORING NORTH OF NEW
ZEALAND. THE FORMATION OF THIS NEW RIDGE IS EXPECTED TO DEFLECT 04P
NORTHWESTWARD. THE CPA TO AMERICAN SAMOA IS ANTICIPATED TO OCCUR
SOMETIME BETWEEN TAU 48 AND TAU 60, ABOUT THE TIME THAT THE
STEERING MECHANISM SHIFTS. A NORTHWESTWARD TRAJECTORY WILL THEN
PERSIST THROUGH THE REMAINDER OF THE FORECAST PERIOD (TAU 72). IN
TERMS OF INTENSITY, 04P IS FORECAST TO MAINTAIN ITS CURRENT
INTENSITY THROUGH TAU 24 DUE PRIMARILY TO WARM SEA SURFACE
TEMPERATURES AND INTERMITTENT CONVECTIVE DEVELOPMENT, WHICH IS 
DEPICTED IN MULTIPLE MESOSCALE MODELS. IF THE CONVECTION FAILS TO 
FORM IN THE FACE OF THE PERSISTENT SHEAR, A QUICKER WEAKENING TREND 
THAN WHAT IS CURRENTLY DEPICTED IN THE JTWC FORECAST COULD OCCUR. 
AFTER TAU 24, DRY AIR ENTRAINMENT INTO THE CORE WILL BECOME 
SIGNIFICANT, CAUSING THE SYSTEM TO STEADILY WEAKEN. NOTABLY, THE 
STRONGEST WINDS ARE EXPECTED TO REMAIN LOCATED WITHIN THE SOUTHERN 
SEMICIRCLE OF THE CIRCULATION THROUGHOUT THE ENTIRE FORECAST PERIOD. 
04P IS FORECAST TO DISSIPATE OVER WATER BY TAU 72, NORTH OF SAMOA. 

MODEL DISCUSSION: MODEL TRACK GUIDANCE IS IN MODERATE AGREEMENT
THROUGH TAU 48 WITH A CROSS-TRACK SPREAD MEASURED TO BE ABOUT 110
NM AT THAT IME. NAVGEM HAS BECOME AN OUTLIER THIS MODEL RUN,
TRACKING THE VORTEX SIGNIFICANTLY FURTHER SOUTH THAN THE REMAINDER
OF GUIDANCE (DISCOUNTED FROM THE TRACKER SPREAD MEASUREMENT). AFTER
TAU 48 MODELS START TO DIVERGE AS THE VORTEX BECOMES VERY SHALLOW.
ECMWF ACTUALLY STALLS THE SYSTEM NEAR AMERICAN SAMOA WHILE UKMET
AND GALWEM RACE IT OFF TO THE NORTHWEST. THE JTWC TRACK FORECAST
REMAINS CONSISTENT WITH THE PREVIOUS FORECAST, PLACED NEAR THE
CONSENSUS THROUGH TAU 48 AND THEN CLOSER TO THE AI-BASED CONSENSUS
THROUGH TAU 72 WITH MEDIUM CONFIDENCE. INTENSITY GUIDANCE IS
SLIGHTLY SPLIT IN THE NEAR TERM, WITH HAFS-A AND COAMPS-TC SHOWING 
SLIGHT INTENSIFICATION THROUGH TAU 24, WHILE THE REMAINDER OF 
GUIDANCE SHOWS WEAKENING STARTING FROM TAU 0. ALL MODELS THEN AGREE 
ON WEAKENING FROM TAU 24 ONWARD, WITH THE EXCEPTION OF HAFS-A, WHICH 
SEEMS UNREALISTIC DUE TO THE AMOUNT OF MID-LEVEL DRY AIR THE SYSTEM 
WILL BE ENCOUNTERING. AS A RESULT, THE JTWC INTENSITY FORECAST IS 
PLACED SLIGHTLY BELOW THE CONSENSUS TO OFFSET HAFS-A.
