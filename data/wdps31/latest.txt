SATELLITE ANALYSIS, INITIAL POSITION AND INTENSITY DISCUSSION:
TROPICAL CYCLONE (TC) 04P MAINTAINS AN IMPRESSIVE SATELLITE
APPEARANCE. ANIMATED MULTISPECTRAL SATELLITE IMAGERY (MSI) DEPICTS
A 10-15 NM WIDE EYE EMBEDDED IN A COMPACT CENTRAL DENSE OVERCAST
(CDO) WITH A RELATIVELY SMOOTH CIRRUS CANOPY. A VERY LARGE BANDING
FEATURE WRAPS AROUND THE EASTERN PERIPHERY. THE SYSTEM MAINTAINS
EXCELLENT EASTWARD AND POLEWARD OUTFLOW, THOUGH MODERATE WESTERLY
SHEAR IS BEGINNING TO RESTRICT WESTWARD AND EQUATORWARD OUTFLOW.
WHILE THE AVERAGE CLOUD TOP TEMPERATURES HAVE WARMED, THE EYE HAS
WARMED TO +17 DEGREES CELSIUS. A 171501Z SSMIS 91 GHZ IMAGE DEPICTS
A HEALTHY RING OF COLD BRIGHTNESS TEMPERATURES ASSOCIATED WITH
HIGHER-END STORMS. ENVIRONMENTAL CONDITIONS ARE STILL FAVORABLE,
CHARACTERIZED BY VERY WARM SEA SURFACE TEMPERATURES (SST) NEAR 30
DEGREES CELSIUS AND MODERATE TO STRONG DIVERGENCE ALOFT. THE
INITIAL POSITION IS PLACED WITH HIGH CONFIDENCE BASED ON THE SMALL,
WELL-DEFINED EYE IN MSI. SUBJECTIVE DVORAK CLASSIFICATIONS HAVE
COME IN AT T6.5-7.0. RECENT D-PRINT AND D-MINT VALUES HAVE HELD
NEAR 130 KTS. THEREFORE, THE INITIAL INTENSITY IS SET AT 130 KTS
WITH MEDIUM CONFIDENCE, MAKING TC XAVIER ONE OF THE MOST INTENSE IN
THE SOUTH PACIFIC FOR THE MONTH OF NOVEMBER.

3. FORECAST REASONING.

SIGNIFICANT FORECAST CHANGES: THERE ARE NO SIGNIFICANT CHANGES TO
THE FORECAST FROM THE PREVIOUS WARNING.

FORECAST DISCUSSION: TC 04P HAS TURNED POLEWARD AS IT ROUNDS THE
NORTHWESTERN PERIPHERY OF THE STR AND WILL CROSS THE RIDGE AXIS BY
TAU 12. AS A RESULT, THE SYSTEM WILL TURN SOUTHEASTWARD AND
MAINTAIN THAT TRAJECTORY THROUGH TAU 48. FORWARD MOTION WILL REMAIN
LOW DURING THIS PERIOD AS THE CIRCULATION MOVES NEAR A LARGE COL
REGION. DURING THIS TIME, THE COMPACT SYSTEM SHOULD STEADILY
WEAKEN. MODERATE WESTERLY SHEAR IS BEGINNING TO RESTRICT WESTWARD
AND EQUATORWARD OUTFLOW. VWS WILL INCREASE TO 20-25 KTS AND REMAIN
WESTERLY, PROMOTING DRY-AIR ENTRAINMENT INTO THE VORTEX. UPWELLING
OF COOLER WATERS MAY ALSO CONTRIBUTE TO THE WEAKENING TREND,
PARTICULARLY WHILE THE SYSTEM REMAINS SLOW MOVING. THE EASTERN
EXTENSION OF THE STR WILL REBUILD POLEWARD OF THE CIRCULATION
FOLLOWING THE PASSAGE OF A DEEP TROUGH BETWEEN TAU 36 AND TAU 60.
CONSEQUENTLY, THE VORTEX SHOULD ABRUPTLY TURN WEST-SOUTHWESTWARD
AND ACCELERATE TOWARD THE VICINITY OF AMERICAN SAMOA. TOWARD THE
END OF THE FORECAST PERIOD, THE SHALLOWER VORTEX SHOULD BEGIN TO
ROUND A LOW- TO MID-LEVEL RIDGE AND TURN POLEWARD. BY TAU 72, THE
SYSTEM WILL ACCELERATE INTO THE SHEAR VECTOR, AND THE VORTEX SHOULD
DECOUPLE AND RAPIDLY WEAKEN.

MODEL DISCUSSION: TRACK GUIDANCE GENERALLY AGREES ON SLOW POLEWARD
TO SOUTHEASTWARD MOTION, FOLLOWED BY A WEST-SOUTHWESTWARD
ACCELERATION. MODEL SPREAD IS HIGH WITH RESPECT TO THE TIMING AND
MAGNITUDE OF THE POLEWARD TURN PRIOR TO THE SHARP DEFLECTION TOWARD
THE WEST-SOUTHWEST. THE JTWC FORECAST TRACK LEANS HEAVILY TOWARD
THE AI-MODEL MEAN (AICN) FOR THE FIRST 60 HOURS, FAVORING THE
SHARPER POLEWARD AI-MODEL SOLUTIONS OVER THE DYNAMICAL MODEL
SOLUTIONS. BEYOND TAU 60, THE JTWC FORECAST HEDGES TOWARD THE
CONVERGENCE OF AICN AND THE MULTI-MODEL CONSENSUS (CONW). THIS
PORTION OF THE TRACK LIES NEAR THE MIDPOINT BETWEEN THE
EXPERIMENTAL AND OPERATIONAL GOOGLE DEEPMIND ENSEMBLE MEANS.
INTENSITY GUIDANCE UNANIMOUSLY DEPICTS STEADY TO RAPID WEAKENING
THROUGH TAU 120. THE JTWC INTENSITY FORECAST IS SET ABOVE DEEPMIND
FOR THE FIRST 12 HOURS AND NEAR DEEPMIND FROM TAU 24 TO TAU 96.
HAFS-A REMAINS A HIGH OUTLIER, INDICATING 60-65 KTS BY TAU 96-120.
AT TAU 120, THE JTWC INTENSITY FORECAST IS BASED ON A BLEND OF THE
50-KT DEEPMIND INTENSITY AND THE 25-35 KT INTENSITY FROM GFS AND
ECMWF.
