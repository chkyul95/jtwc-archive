SATELLITE ANALYSIS, INITIAL POSITION AND INTENSITY DISCUSSION: 
ANIMATED MULTISPECTRAL SATELLITE IMAGERY (MSI) DEPICTS A RAPIDLY 
DETERIORATING TROPICAL CYCLONE (TC), CHARACTERIZED BY A CONTRACTING 
AND ASYMMETRIC CENTRAL DENSE OVERCAST (CDO). WHILE THERE IS NO 
DISTINCT EYE EVIDENT IN THE MSI OR ENHANCED INFRARED (EIR) IMAGERY, A 
REMNANT UPPER-LEVEL CYCLONIC ROTATION IS COLOCATED WITH A SUBTLE WARM 
TEMPERATURE ANOMALY IN THE EIR, SUPPORTING THE POSITION OF THE UPPER-
LEVEL VORTEX CENTER. A LACK OF RECENT MICROWAVE IMAGERY, OTHER THAN 
COARSE-RESOLUTION SOUNDERS, PRECLUDES A HIGH-CONFIDENCE ASSESSMENT OF 
THE MAGNITUDE OF VERTICAL VORTEX TILT; HOWEVER, A TROPICS MILLIMETER-
WAVE SOUNDER (TMS06) PASS REVEALED THAT THE REMNANT MICROWAVE EYE IS 
CONFINED TO THE UPPER TROPOSPHERE (205 GHZ ICE-SCATTERING CHANNEL) AND 
IS NO LONGER APPARENT IN THE MID-LEVEL 183 GHZ WATER VAPOR CHANNEL, 
INDICATING THAT VERTICAL VORTEX DECOUPLING IS UNDERWAY. THE INITIAL 
POSITION IS ASSESSED WITH LOW CONFIDENCE DUE TO AN OBSCURED LOW-LEVEL 
CIRCULATION CENTER (LLCC) AND THE ABSENCE OF RECENT MICROWAVE IMAGERY. 
THE INITIAL INTENSITY IS ASSESSED WITH MEDIUM CONFIDENCE, BASED ON A 
BLEND OF THE SUBJECTIVE AND OBJECTIVE INTENSITY ESTIMATES OUTLINED 
BELOW. OF NOTE, WHILE DVORAK CURRENT INTENSITY (CI) VALUES REMAIN 
ELEVATED DUE TO WEAKENING CONSTRAINTS, FINAL-T AND RAW-T NUMBERS ARE 
DROPPING RAPIDLY, RANGING BETWEEN T4.4 AND T4.8. ENVIRONMENTAL 
CONDITIONS ARE UNFAVORABLE, CHARACTERIZED BY RAPIDLY INCREASING DEEP-
LAYER VERTICAL WIND SHEAR (AND STRONGER MID-LEVEL SHEAR), MODERATE 
POLEWARD OUTFLOW, INCREASING MID-TROPOSPHERIC DRY AIR INTRUSION, AND 
MARGINALLY WARM SEA SURFACE TEMPERATURES (SSTS).

INITIAL WIND RADII BASIS: PERSISTENCE 

CURRENT STEERING MECHANISM: WEAK STEERING PATTERN.

3. FORECAST REASONING.

SIGNIFICANT FORECAST CHANGES: THE POST-TAU 72 FORECAST TRACK HAS 
SHIFTED NORTHWESTWARD BY APPROXIMATELY 165 NM AND NOW DIRECTS THE 
CENTER OF TC 04P NORTH OF AMERICAN SAMOA IN THE EXTENDED-RANGE 
FORECAST. 

FORECAST DISCUSSION: WHILE STILL POSITIONED WITHIN A WEAK DEEP-LAYER 
STEERING COL, TC 04P HAS BEGUN TURNING POLEWARD AS THE VORTEX REDUCES 
IN VERTICAL EXTENT. THE TRACK FORECAST FROM THIS POINT ONWARD IS 
TIGHTLY COUPLED WITH THE INTENSITY AND VORTEX DEPTH EVOLUTION. BOTH 
THE CURRENT CIMSS SHEAR ANALYSIS (22 KNOTS) AND HIGH-RESOLUTION MODEL-
DERIVED SOUNDINGS INDICATE THAT SHEAR MAGNITUDES HAVE DOUBLED WITHIN 
THE PAST SIX HOURS. HAFS-A VERTICAL CROSS-SECTIONS DEPICT THE ONSET OF 
RAPID VERTICAL VORTEX DECOUPLING WITHIN THE NEXT 6-12 HOURS, AFTER 
WHICH THE REMNANT VORTEX WILL BE CONFINED TO THE LOW- TO MID-LEVELS OF 
THE TROPOSPHERE AND GOVERNED BY THE GENERALLY EAST-NORTHEASTERLY 
STEERING FLOW ALONG THE NORTHERN PERIPHERY OF THE EXPANSIVE 
SUBTROPICAL RIDGE (STR) TO THE SOUTHEAST. THE SYSTEM WILL DEFLECT 
SHARPLY SOUTHWESTWARD AFTER TAU 12 AND MAINTAIN A SOUTHWESTWARD 
TRAJECTORY THROUGH TAU 72. BY TAU 72, THE SYSTEM WILL HAVE WEAKENED 
SUFFICIENTLY THAT THE VORTEX WILL BE CONFINED TO THE LOWER TROPOSPHERE 
(BELOW 850 MB) AND STEERED BY THE LOW-LEVEL TRADE- WIND FLOW, WHICH 
WILL SHIFT FROM EASTERLY TO EAST-SOUTHEASTERLY BY THE END OF THE 
FORECAST PERIOD. IN RESPONSE, TC 04P WILL TURN WESTWARD, THEN WEST-
NORTHWESTWARD AFTER TAU 72. THIS TRAJECTORY WILL CARRY THE CENTER OF 
THE WEAKENING SYSTEM TO THE NORTH OF THE MAIN ISLANDS OF AMERICAN 
SAMOA AND SAMOA FROM TAU 96 THROUGH TAU 120. AS THE SYSTEM WEAKENS, 
THE SURFACE WIND FIELD WILL BECOME INCREASINGLY ASYMMETRIC, ESPECIALLY 
AFTER TAU 72, WITH THE STRONGEST GRADIENT WINDS CONFINED TO THE 
SOUTHERN SEMICIRCLE. THE FORECAST CALLS FOR TC 04P TO DISSIPATE AS A 
DISCRETE TROPICAL CYCLONE NO LATER THAN TAU 120.  

MODEL DISCUSSION: DETERMINISTIC AND ENSEMBLE TRACK GUIDANCE IS IN 
OVERALL AGREEMENT ON THE SYNOPTIC TRACK SCENARIO, WITH ALL MEMBERS OF 
THE TRACK CONSENSUS NOW SUPPORTING A SOUTHWESTWARD TRAJECTORY THROUGH 
TAU 72, FOLLOWED BY A WESTWARD SHIFT THEREAFTER. DISCOUNTING NAVGEM, 
WHICH UNREALISTICALLY ADVECTS THE SYSTEM STRAIGHT SOUTHWESTWARD INTO 
THE RIDGE, THE REMAINDER OF THE GUIDANCE PACKAGE IS CONSTRAINED WITHIN 
A 100 NM WIDE ENVELOPE THROUGH TAU 72. THE GEFS, GFS, AND EC-AIFS 
DEFINE THE NORTHERN BOUNDARY OF THE GUIDANCE ENVELOPE, WHILE GALWEM 
AND THE ECEPS MEAN ARE POSITIONED ON THE SOUTHERN EDGE. BEYOND TAU 72, 
CROSS-TRACK SPREAD INCREASES TO 180 NM, MAINTAINING THE SAME RELATIVE 
MODEL ORIENTATION. CRITICALLY, BOTH THE GOOGLE DEEPMIND (GDMI) AND 
ECMWF DETERMINISTIC TRACKERS HAVE SHIFTED SIGNIFICANTLY (165 NM) 
EQUATORWARD SINCE THE 1800Z MODEL CYCLE, AND ARE NOW POSITIONED NORTH 
OF THE CONSENSUS MEAN WITHIN THE NORTHERN HALF OF THE GUIDANCE 
ENVELOPE. THE OFFICIAL JTWC FORECAST IS PLACED NEAR THE MODIFIED 
CONSENSUS MEAN (OMITTING NAVGEM) AND THE GEFS MEAN, JUST SOUTH OF THE 
AI CONSENSUS (AICN), ECMWF, AND GDMI. CONFIDENCE IS LOW AFTER TAU 72, 
PARTICULARLY IN LIGHT OF THE SUBSTANTIAL SPREAD AMONG THE ENSEMBLES, 
WHICH DEPICT A LARGE DEGREE OF INTRA- AND INTER-ENSEMBLE MEMBER 
DISPERSION. INTENSITY GUIDANCE IS IN GOOD AGREEMENT THAT THE SYSTEM 
WILL WEAKEN THROUGH THE FORECAST PERIOD, THOUGH NOTABLE DIFFERENCES 
EXIST REGARDING THE RATE OF DECAY. DECAY-SHIPS (GFS AND NAVGEM) ARE 
THE SLOWEST TO WEAKEN TC 04P, WHILE THE GDMI AND HWRF ARE THE MOST 
AGGRESSIVE, AND COAMPS-TC (COTC) AND HAFS-A DEPICT AN INTERMEDIATE 
WEAKENING RATE. THE JTWC INTENSITY FORECAST IS PLACED NEAR THE MULTI-
MODEL INTENSITY CONSENSUS (ICNW) THROUGH THE FORECAST PERIOD WITH 
MEDIUM CONFIDENCE.
